Pick a package to open its steps, or create your own. Each package is a board of step cards: frames are the phases, arrows give the order, and cards stack the same way as on the Whiteboard (3 per card, 4 levels).

Getting started
  • Each card is a step · double-clickdouble-tap the board for a new one · clicktap a card to edit, double-clickdouble-tap to type on it
  • Drag a card onto another’s bottom edge to tuck it in as a sub-step or note (up to 4 levels)
  • Drag the blue dot on a selected card’s edge to draw an arrow to the next step · the frame button makes a frame for a phase
  • Right-clickPress and hold anything for options · File to export, import or print · ? for shortcuts
