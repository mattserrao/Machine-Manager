## Reading the survey numbers
- Press felts at end of life: air perm 18-19 cfm, caliper 2.48-2.54 mm; at 5.9 wk they read 24-26 cfm and 2.79-2.84 mm (1300/1500 gsm and 1400/1600 gsm felts) [DOC HF-SV-260121 p.1] [DOC MP-SV-260304 p.1]
- Forming fabric end of life: 89% wear layer, 441-452 cfm; at 5.9 wk 54% wear, about 480-490 cfm [DOC NF-SV-251210 p.1] [DOC NF-SV-260121 p.1]
- Dryer fabrics: air perm falls from about 74-93 cfm new to 64-67 cfm at replacement [DOC NF-SV-260304 p.1] [DOC CT-SV-260527 p.1]
- Refiner plates: bar wear 53-64% and 61.3-63.0 kWh/t normal; 85% and 65.4 kWh/t abnormal (REF 2, 09/30/2026) [DOC RR-PL-260930-B p.1]
