## Reference table SM-REF-03 "Grade speeds and production"
- 24#: 2632 fpm, basis weight 25.52 lb/1000 ft2, full rate 34.8 t/h [DOC SM-REF-03 p.2]
- 28#: 2262 fpm, 28.03 lb/1000 ft2, 32.8 t/h [DOC SM-REF-03 p.2]
- 31#: 2147 fpm, 31.1 lb/1000 ft2, 34.6 t/h [DOC SM-REF-03 p.2]
- 34 Super: 2027 fpm, 34.01 lb/1000 ft2, 35.7 t/h [DOC SM-REF-03 p.2]
- Speed falls as basis weight rises; the full rate stays 32.8-35.7 t/h across grades [DOC SM-REF-03 p.2]
- Units: speed fpm, basis weight lb/1000 ft2, rate t/h (tons per hour); the RCFA "Production loss" values are in t and the downtime values in h, so loss divided by hours gives about 31 t/h on the RCFAs (computed, for example 126 t / 4.0 h = 31.5 t/h [RCFA-SM-25-001] and 294 t / 9.5 h = 30.9 t/h [RCFA-SM-26-006])
- Observed in the daily reports (single-grade days, Nov 2025 - Oct 2026): 24# averages 2,564 fpm and 684 t/day over 196 days; 34 Super 2,000 fpm and 720 t/day over 40 days; daily tons, steam t/t, kWh/t and OME by month are in [[production-daily-reports]].
