## Operative values (verbatim)
- Intensity: "I = (Applied Motor Power - No-Load) / [RPM x Bar Edge Length x (min/60s)]", unit Ws/m; N = (RPM x Bar Edge Length) / Tons per Day; P = I x N = Net Power / (t/d) = net hpd/t [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.23] [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.24]
- Throughput: "short t/d = Flow (gpm) x 6 x % Consistency"; "metric t/h = Flow (lpm) x 0.06 x %Consistency"; example 500 gpm x 6 x 0.045 = 135 st/d [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.25]
- Example: motor 575 hp, no-load 115 hp, net 460 hp / 135 t/d = 3.4 hpd/t; metric 342 net kW / 3.8 t/h = 90 kWh/t [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.25]
- Pulp consistency in stock prep refining normally 2 to 6% [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.14]
- CSF: 1 L at 0.3% consistency; up to 750 ml unrefined unbleached softwood kraft, 30-40 ml fine groundwood; kraft stock prep refining typically 600 to 250 ml [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.15]
- Pulp yields (Table 3): GWD and RMP 93-98%; TMP 94-96%; CTMP 85-94%; semi-chemical 65-90%; high yield kraft 55-65%; bleached HW kraft 48-50%; bleached SW kraft 42-45% [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.10]
- Table 4 net hpd/t: fine paper HWD kraft 2-5, SWD kraft 3-7, tickler 1-1.5; linerboard base 5-7, top 10-12; news SWD kraft 2-5, TMP/GWD 1-5; GWD printing paper SWD kraft 3-7, TMP/GWD 3-6 (table layout garbled in the source, columns inferred) [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.26]
- Table 5 freeness drop per net hpd/t: bleached SWD kraft 20-40 ml; bleached HWD kraft 60-100; GWD 3-7; OCC 40-70; mixed office waste 50-70; news 20-35 [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.26]
- "available power for refining be around 25% greater than the expected nominal level" [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.26]
- Table 6 refining intensity Ws/m: SWD kraft 1.0-2.5; HWD kraft 0.1-0.5; recycle 0.2-0.8; TMP/GWD 0.2-0.5 [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.28]
- Unbleached kraft sack paper: tear gain with refining only assured if intensity "sufficiently low (i.e. 1.5-2.0 Ws/m)"; many SW refiners operate at 2.0-4.0 Ws/m, hardwood 0.6-1.0 Ws/m; plate-technology lower limit was 0.6-0.8, now 0.1-0.5 Ws/m [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.27]
- Reduced periphery plates: "No load power = k * diameter^4.3 * rpm^3" [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.28]
- Table 7 (reduced active diameter, hp saved, annual saving at $0.05/kWh): 46 to 43 in, 83 hp, $27,120; 46 to 40, 150 hp, $49,000; 42 to 39, 90 hp, $29,400; 38 to 35, 65 hp, $21,240; 34 to 31, 45 hp, $14,700; 30 to 27, 45 hp, $14,700; 26 to 24, 33 hp, $10,800 [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.29]
- Bar width minimum practical "in excess of 0.050"", baling-wire risk "in the order of 0.075""; groove width: groundwood post-refining 0.050", hardwood at least 0.075", softwood at least 0.090" or 0.125" [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.29]
- Table 8 double disc capacity (plate dia in, max hp, rotor rpm, nominal no-load hp, gpm low/med/high), examples: 20 in 300 hp 720 rpm 75 hp 150/250/400; 30 in 600 hp 600 rpm 125 hp 375/600/1100; 34 in 800 hp 514 rpm 135 hp 475/750/1400 (also 1000 hp 600 rpm 215 hp 550/875/1650); 46 in 2000 hp 450 rpm 325 hp 1025/1675/3275; 54 in 3000 hp 400 rpm 450 hp 1475/2425/4850 [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.30]
- "In most instances, the high end of these ranges is very optimistic and will result in poor refining with very short useful plate life." [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.30]
- No-load: "NL = 102 x (RPM/100)^3 x (Da/100)^4.3 x (2 x Gw /( Bw + Gw)) x (Gd /4)" (NL hp; Da active diameter in; Gw, Bw, Gd in sixteenths of an inch) [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.33]
- No-load can fall 60-80% lower as plates wear from new to fully worn; "no-load is not dependent on the weight or mass of the rotating elements" [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.32]
- Appendix C case: 34 in DD, 1000 hp/600 rpm motor, 700 hp load, 900 gpm, 4%, pattern 2.0/2.0/4.0, 30 km/rev: no-load 213 hp, net 487 hp (363 kW), 216 t/d, 2.3 net hpd/t, SEL 1.2 Ws/m, inlet CSF 625 to 500 = 55 ml per hpd/t (typical HWD kraft 60-100) [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.40] [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.41]
- Same case, 31 in reduced periphery Finebar plates (bar 1.0, groove 1.5, depth 3.5 sixteenths, 59 km/rev): no-load 150 hp (63 hp saved), SEL 0.6 Ws/m, about $18,400 per year at $0.045/kWh [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.42]
- CONFLICT inside the manual: HWD intensity range is 0.1-0.5 Ws/m in Table 6 [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.28] but "recommended SEL range for HWD of 0.3-0.8 Ws/m" in the case study [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.42]; and mills commonly run 0.6-1.0 Ws/m [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.27]
- Pump-through duo-flo: two disc pairs behave as parallel pumps; keep pressure rise about 1.5 bar or less, use recycle flow at low net flow; 0.1 bar rotor-face difference can shift about 40 kW between pairs [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.36] [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.37]
