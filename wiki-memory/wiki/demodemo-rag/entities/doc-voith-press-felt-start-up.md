## Start-up sequence (verbatim)
1. "Make certain the fabric is square to the machine and the tradeline is parallel to the CD" [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.2]
2. "Adjust the stretch roll until the fabric is snug" [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.2]
3. Jog with nip open to inspect and center; if not possible "load to dead weight and jog for inspection" [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.2]
4. "Stop the press and increase felt tension to normal running range" [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.2]
5. "Turn on the lube shower and with the nip in dead weight load crawl the felt until it has been uniformly wet in." [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.2]
6. "When the felt is uniformly wet, load the press to normal load and turn on the uhle box vacuum pump. (full vacuum potential should be utilized)" [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.3]
7. "Maintain tension during the startup and check frequently" [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.3]

## Notes and showers
- Bring press up to speed and run 15-20 minutes prior to tailing; adjust anti-blow box as needed [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.3]
- "Startup high pressure shower should not exceed 200 psi. (150 would be ideal)" [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.3]
- "Lube shower pressure should be regulated to provide single coverage" [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.3]
- Lube shower: 15-25 psi, continuous, .04-.08 gpm/in. of fabric width, 3-6 in. spacing, stationary 90-120 degree spray, single coverage [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.4]
- High pressure shower: 175-250 psi, continuous, .05-.10 gpm/in. of fabric width, oscillating [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.4]
- "Shower Oscillation Rate (inches/minute) = Felt Speed (fpm) X Shower Nozzle Size (inch) / Felt Length (feet)" [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.4]
