## Operative values (verbatim)
- Lignin is enriched on unbleached kraft fibre surfaces; "The fraction of surface lignin removed by oxygen or hydrogen peroxide is much smaller than the total decrease in lignin content." [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.1]
- Oxygen delignification "reduces the total lignin content by about 50%" but surface lignin by only about 15%; ozone removes surface lignin about as effectively as bulk; peroxide hardly affects surface lignin (OPZEP: about 35% total, about 10% surface). [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.12-13]
- Extractives: bulk 0.09-0.17 w-%, surface coverage printed as "between 11 and 1.7 %" (OCR; probably 1.1-1.7%), "in some cases up to 100 times higher than in the bulk"; oxygen stage strongly reduces them, ozone and ClO2 do not, peroxide always does. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.14-15]
- Two acid types: pK about 3.3-3.4 (uronic, hexenuronic) and pK about 5.5 (weaker, tracks residual lignin). [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.2, p.18]
- Table 1 (total charge ueq/g, acids umol/g): unbleached kappa 17.7 = 70/65; kappa 56.6 = 130/115; O-stage kappa 13.2 = 110/90; OPZEP and DEDED kappa 0.8 = 55/40. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.18]
- Hexenuronic acid is decomposed by chlorine, chlorine dioxide and ozone but unreactive in peroxide and oxygen. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.19-20]
- Water adhesion rises slightly with delignification in the cook and "strongly after oxygen delignification". [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.2, p.16]
- Charge correlates linearly with WRV, wet fibre flexibility, relative bonded area (RBA) and tensile strength; chain: more charge, more swelling, more flexibility, denser sheet, higher RBA and tensile. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.23, p.28]
- Intrinsic bond strength (Page equation): bleached hardwood about 3.7 N/mm2 vs softwood about 2.2 N/mm2; Scott bond/RBA about 370 J/m2 (hardwood) and 460 J/m2 (softwood), independent of charge. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.26]
- "40-60 % of the swelling seems to be due to other factors" than ionizable groups. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.27]
- Beating: bonding develops faster the more ionizable groups the pulp has; charge does not change creation of new surface during refining (0.5 M NaCl test). [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.25]
- Kappa to lignin: L (%) = kappa / 6.546; lab cook EA 20-26%, sulfidity 30%, L/W 5.0, to 173 C in 80 min. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.5, p.11]
