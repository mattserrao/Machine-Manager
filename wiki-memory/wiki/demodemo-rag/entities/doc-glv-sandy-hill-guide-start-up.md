## Operative values (verbatim steps)
- "For initial start-up and after fabric changes, the machine section should be started slowly and the guiding system checked regularly for proper operation as the speed increases." [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- Step 1: "Lock the auto guide actuator housing in the middle position with the locking pin." [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- Step 2: set regulator air pressure per Table 4-1; for inclined or vertical mounting "increase air pressure until the diaphragms balance the weight of the guide roll in the center of auto guide travel. This occurs when the locking pin can be pulled out" [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- Table 4-1 (psi), columns: both diaphragms variable at filter/regulator min-max | variable diaphragm min-max | constant diaphragm at diaphragm min-max [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- Horizontal: 30-75 | 30-75 | 30-40 [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- Vertical: 60-75 | 60-75 | 0 (Top) - 0 (Top) [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- At angle: 30-75 | 30-45 to 75 | 30-75 [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- Hand guide: adjust it to bring the auto guide to centre of travel ("The auto guide must operate in the center of its travel"); then remove the locking pin and store it in the lower hole of the auto guide base [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- Position the controller so the palm is "centered on and perpendicular to the fabric at the desired fabric position" [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- Direction test: force the palm away from the fabric; "The fabric should move in the opposite direction. If the fabric follows the palm, switch the piping connections to the auto guide." [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
