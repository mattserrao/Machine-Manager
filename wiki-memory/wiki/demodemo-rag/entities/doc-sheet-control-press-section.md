## Operative values (verbatim)
- "The transfer roll or pick-up suction roll needs to have an even front to back penetration depth into forming fabric of approximately 0.5 inch (12.5 mm). Too low of penetration supports edge drop-off and too high can lead to trim stealing." Target wrap 4-6 inches (10-15 cm). [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.7]
- "Near 15% solids or less, the squirts have difficulty cutting a clean edge." [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.6]
- "Typically, a 1% change in former exit solids ends up being nearly a 10% change in gpm of water coming into the first nip." [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.5]
- Gap before nip: "about .75 inch (19mm.) is typically adequate" (Rex Vass, Asia); OEM designers allow 10 degrees of separation on the inlet run [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.8]
- Uhle boxes: "no control to keep the vacuum levels below some target level like 16-18 inches Hg (54-61 kPa) depending on the fineness of the batt denier" [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.19]
- Conclusions: "Vacuum control active if exceeding 18 in. Hg (61 kPa) levels"; HP showers 3-4 inches from the felt surface [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.24]
- Fine paper and coated board HP showers 150 psi (10.3 bar) to 250 psi (17.2 bar); unbleached packaging "typically ran over 300 psi (20.7 bar)" [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.19]
- "you would want the felt that the sheet should follow to have one or two steps finer denier than the opposite felt" [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.19]
- Lange rule 15: paper grades "less than ~90 g/m2 will stick to the underside of a pick-up felt between the suction pickup and the 1st press"; rule 16: "Board grades must be supported by the bottom felt immediately after the suction pickup roll" [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.25]
