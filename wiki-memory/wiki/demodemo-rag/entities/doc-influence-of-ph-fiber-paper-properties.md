## Method (verbatim values)
- Pulps from Gruvon, never-dried: NSSC 1.10 mm / 29.5 um / pH 5.2; BSW 2.14 mm / 31.0 um / pH 5.3; BHW 0.91 mm / 22.0 um / pH 3.5 [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.26]
- pH adjusted with HCl or NaOH to 3, 5, 7, 9; PFI beating unbeaten, 2000 or 4000 rev; "36 experimental combinations"; ionic strength left unregulated [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.27]
- Pulps left overnight after pH adjustment [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.27]
- Lab sheets 140 g/m2 (not standard 60) "to be in the same range as the grammage of fluting and liner"; stock 1.5% kraft / 2% NSSC; pressed 2.8 bar 5.5 min then 2.8 bar 2.2 min [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.28]
- Tests: SR (ISO 5267-1), WRV (ISO 23714), charge by Mutek PCD with poly-DADMAC, tensile (ISO 1924-3), SCT (ISO 9895), CCT30 (ISO 16945, 100 mm pieces) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.28-29]

## Mill trial (PM6, NSSC)
- pH changed "with addition of concentrated sulfuric acid in the machine chest"; specific refining energy kept constant [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.30]
- Samples at pH 5.6 and 4.8; lower limit set to 4 because of "a high risk of formation of hydrogen sulfide gas at low pH levels"; trial "discontinued at pH 4.8 due to formation of sulfur dioxide gas" [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.30]
- Result: no significant difference in tensile, stiffness or compressive index between pH 4.8 and 5.6; only edge crush index of beaten pulp differed (Table 5, A10: CCT 21.8 vs 26.3 Nm/g beaten) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.43]
- Why: at pKa 4, about 86% (pH 4.8) vs 98% (pH 5.6) of carboxyl groups dissociated, "approximately same region" [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.41]
- Not directly comparable to lab: mill pulp saw new pH 10-20 min before beating, lab pulp overnight [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.43]
- Machine-made paper MD/CD results over pH 4.4-5.7 in Table A11 show no trend (e.g. MD tensile index 114-128 kNm/kg) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.53]

## Key results (lab)
- NSSC surface charge unbeaten: pH 3 55, pH 5 81, pH 7 83, pH 9 103 ueq/g [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.32]
- BSW charge pH 3 0.4 to pH 9 3.3 ueq/g; BHW pH 3 2.4 to pH 9 15 ueq/g (measurement uncertain) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.35, p.38]
- NSSC 2000 rev tensile index: pH 3 71.1, pH 5 86.3, pH 7 83.1, pH 9 81.6 kNm/kg; CCT30 pH 3 26.7 vs pH 7 30.5 Nm/g [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.50]
- NSSC 2000 rev WRV: pH 3 2.48, pH 9 2.90 g/g [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.50]
- BSW 4000 rev tensile index: pH 3 90.6, pH 9 105.1 kNm/kg; SCT pH 3 30.5, pH 9 33.7 kNm/kg [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.51]
- BHW tensile index 92.7-96.5 kNm/kg at all pH, no significant difference [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.52]
- NSSC 4000 rev: no pH effect, "probably due to too extensive beating" (fines) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.34]
- Pre-study: NSSC charge rose 102 to about 125 ueq/g over 24 h at pH 9; WRV NSSC 1.91 to 2.05 g/g over 24 h [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.49]

## Conclusions (p.44)
- "Bleached hardwood pulp was not affected by changes in pH." "Unbeaten pulps was not affected by changes in pH." "A higher pH level during beating increases the rate of beating for NSSC and BSW." [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.44]
