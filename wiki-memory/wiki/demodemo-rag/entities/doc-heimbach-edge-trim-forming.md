## Operative values (verbatim)
- "The usual pressure for edge trims ranges from 15 to 40 bar. The distance between nozzle and sheet should be approx. 70-100 mm." [DOC dcdb0d87-3ad5-4f05-8bb0-5454dea7c99c p.2]
- Hand stroboscope "set at a frequency of approx. 50 – 60 Hz" [DOC dcdb0d87-3ad5-4f05-8bb0-5454dea7c99c p.2]
- "For nozzles with the usual jet diameters of 0.4 to 0.5 mm a filter element with a mesh of maximum 200 μm is necessary. An efficient pre-filtration (50 μm or less) prior to the edge trim pump is also recommended." [DOC dcdb0d87-3ad5-4f05-8bb0-5454dea7c99c p.3]
- "Good experiences have been made with water temperatures from 50º to 60º Celsius." [DOC dcdb0d87-3ad5-4f05-8bb0-5454dea7c99c p.3]
- "At machine speeds up to approx. 500 m/min the use of single-jet nozzles on both single-layer and multi-layer fabrics is generally adequate" [DOC dcdb0d87-3ad5-4f05-8bb0-5454dea7c99c p.4]
- MD angle "between 15 ° and 40 ° from the vertical"; CD angle "about 3 ° - 12 °" [DOC dcdb0d87-3ad5-4f05-8bb0-5454dea7c99c p.5]
- Worked example: 0.4 mm nozzle at 20 bar gives about 2600 m/min jet speed; machine at 1200 m/min; angle about 28 deg to the vertical [DOC dcdb0d87-3ad5-4f05-8bb0-5454dea7c99c p.5]
