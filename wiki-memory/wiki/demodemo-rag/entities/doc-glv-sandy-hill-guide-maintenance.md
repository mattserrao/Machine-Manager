## Operative values
- "The maximum recommended life for the needle bearings is three (3) years and they should be inspected for wear at least once a year." [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.1]
- Check guide operation with machine down "by pulling the palm back and forth" to verify smooth movement and correct response [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.1]
- A worn palm "may often be turned upside down to gain a new wear surface"; check palm for wear and sharp edges that could damage the fabric [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.1]
- Inspect rubber air diaphragms for cuts, blisters, cracks; air leaks cause poor guiding and possible failure [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.1]
- "performed a minimum of once a year": 1 rebuild and recalibrate controller; 2 inspect needle bearings, replace if necessary [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.1]
- 3 check ways for wear and position, rotate end caps 180 degrees for a new way surface; 4 inspect air diaphragms; 5 controller palm; 6 air connections and lines (cuts, cracks, kinks); 7 slide blocks in hand guide for base wear [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.2]
- "WARNING! All maintenance procedures should be performed with the machine down. Follow proper Lockout and Tagging Procedure." [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.1]
- Some auto guides run for years with no maintenance, which "will eventually lead to unscheduled machine down time"; schedules should vary with load, speed, vibration, temperature, atmosphere [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.1]
