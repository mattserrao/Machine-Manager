## Operative values (verbatim)
- Problems on low/medium/high vacuum dewatering elements "are noted as problems on 90 percent of systems studied" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.2]
- "It is not unusual to find that one low vacuum element is unknowingly removing 10-30 percent of the total headbox flow." [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.3]
- Max vacuum of a low-vac element: measure the elevation of the vacuum outlet above the seal pot whitewater overflow, "as little as 12" to as much as 40"" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.3]
- "Generally, flatboxes do not need more than five or six slots and slot widths are usually decreased as the sheet approaches the couch." [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.4]
- Fewer flatboxes plus low-drag ceramics (silicon nitride) "can easily lead to 10-20 percent power being saved among couch, wire turning roll, and other former drives." [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.4]
- Uhle box excess slot area is a problem on "50 percent of systems studied" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.4]
- "Slot area is based only on machine speed. For example, a total slot width of 1" is required for a machine speed of 2500 fpm." Reference: TAPPI TIP 0404-27 [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- "A vacuum factor range is 15-20 cubic feet per minute, per square inch of slot area (cfm/in )"; a 250"-wide, 2500 fpm machine with 1" total slot width needs 4000-5000 cfm per uhle box [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- "Minimize uhle box slots to not exceed 2 milliseconds of dwell time per uhle box" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.6]
- Seal water: standard flow "can vary by plus or minus 10-20 percent with little impact on performance" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.7]
- "There are often opportunities to lower vacuum system operating power by 10-20 percent through analysis and optimization." [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.8]
- Greatest improvement found: 360" printing paper machine, "1.2 megawatts from the vacuum system - a 40 percent horsepower reduction" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.9]
