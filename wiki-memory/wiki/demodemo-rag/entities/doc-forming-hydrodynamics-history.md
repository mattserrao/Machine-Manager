## Operative values quoted
- "Cc can be as small as 0.62." [DOC 8e89cddd-56fe-4ef8-9aca-84a8ffe2a0f6 p.2]
- "at about 900 m/min, suction would equal atmospheric pressure." [DOC 8e89cddd-56fe-4ef8-9aca-84a8ffe2a0f6 p.4]
- Roll-blade consistency after initial roll dewatering about 1.5 to 2.0%; conventional roll former about 6%. [DOC 8e89cddd-56fe-4ef8-9aca-84a8ffe2a0f6 p.11]
- Two edge pulses merge into one at blade width about 10 mm. [DOC 8e89cddd-56fe-4ef8-9aca-84a8ffe2a0f6 p.14]
- Fig. 19 (Zhao and Kerekes 1995): peak about 3300 N/m2 at 8 m/s to about 7500 N/m2 at 18 m/s. [DOC 8e89cddd-56fe-4ef8-9aca-84a8ffe2a0f6 p.14]
- Fig. 17 (Brauns 1986) conditions: 3000 ft/min, 25 pli wire tension, 35.5 lb/3000 ft2, 50 gpm per inch; pulses at about 3, 8 and 14 inches. [DOC 8e89cddd-56fe-4ef8-9aca-84a8ffe2a0f6 p.12]
- Fourdriniers to about 1200 m/min (Holik 2010). [DOC 8e89cddd-56fe-4ef8-9aca-84a8ffe2a0f6 p.6]
- DOC b0da1bd5 wording: "This consistency has been found to be around 1.5 to 2.0%. In contrast, the typical consistency leaving the forming roll of a roll former is 6%." [DOC b0da1bd5-dc7f-41ae-8761-601919ce49fe p.11]
- DOC b0da1bd5 wording: "pressure pulses on each edge of a wide blade converge into one pulse when blade width is reduced to about 10 mm" [DOC b0da1bd5-dc7f-41ae-8761-601919ce49fe p.14]
- Modern machines: "over a billion fibres per second ... paper five fibre diameters thick ... speeds over 100 km/h." [DOC b0da1bd5-dc7f-41ae-8761-601919ce49fe p.16]
- Text says "Equation 7 shows the influence of key factors in blade forming" on p.14, but the blade pressure equation is Eq. 8 (Eq. 7 is the roll-former Bernoulli relation); both copies flag this. [DOC b0da1bd5-dc7f-41ae-8761-601919ce49fe p.14]
