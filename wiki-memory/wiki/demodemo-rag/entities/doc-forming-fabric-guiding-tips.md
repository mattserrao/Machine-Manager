## Operative statements
- "The fabric will guide toward the side of the guide roll that it touches first." [DOC 59151609-8cbd-461a-874e-aaeb006dfc27 p.1]
- Fourdrinier (long fabrics): "a wrap angle of at least 25°" needed; top/gap formers can be a little less [DOC 59151609-8cbd-461a-874e-aaeb006dfc27 p.3]
- Polyamide CD yarns: friction coefficient with guide roll "about 30% less than with polyester yarns" [DOC 59151609-8cbd-461a-874e-aaeb006dfc27 p.3]
- Tension: check "on both the drive and tending sides at the same position (e.g. before the guide roll)" [DOC 59151609-8cbd-461a-874e-aaeb006dfc27 p.2]
- Low friction fixes: increase wrap angle, guide roll diameter or roll softness; limit water on and around the guide roll [DOC 59151609-8cbd-461a-874e-aaeb006dfc27 p.3]
- Drilled suction box covers: best fix is slotted covers; else alternate drill angle, with "the last suction box in the running direction" drilled opposite to the fabric twill angle [DOC 59151609-8cbd-461a-874e-aaeb006dfc27 p.4]
- Summary checklist: long-term issue, check drilled covers vs twill, guide roll location/wrap/alignment, reduce lubrication showers near guide roll; just started, check guide palm, guide assembly stroke, wire tension, roll and box alignment; stock on/off, choose fabric design [DOC 59151609-8cbd-461a-874e-aaeb006dfc27 p.4]
