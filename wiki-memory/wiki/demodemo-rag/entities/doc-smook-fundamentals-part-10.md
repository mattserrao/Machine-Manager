## Operative values (quoted)
- "A value of about 1.3 kg steam per kg water is typical of a modern, well-designed, well-maintained system." [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.7]
- Hot pressing: sheet 60 to 90 C; "every 10 C rise in sheet temperature adds about 1 percentage point of dryness after the last press nip" [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.2]
- Traversing HP shower 1.0 to 1.5 gpm at 1000 psig [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.4]
- Rimming "usually starts between about 1200 and 1400 ft/min" [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.10]
- Blow-through typically 15-20% of condensate mass flow [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.10]
- About 10 scans register 90% of a profile step change [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.20]
- Winder drive speed 2-1/2 to 3 times the paper machine [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.25]
