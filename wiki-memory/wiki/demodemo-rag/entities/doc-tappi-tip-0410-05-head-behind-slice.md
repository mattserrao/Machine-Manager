## Operative values (verbatim)
- "Jet velocity may be determined from the energy balance by measuring pressure between conditions at the pressure tap to those in the free jet." [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.1]
- Vj = sqrt(2 g H) or Vj = K sqrt(H); "Note H is not just the pressure transmitter reading, but includes also velocity head and elevation head as well." [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.2]
- First-order estimate: velocity head often neglected but "the elevation head should be included". [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.2]
- Table 1 K values: 138.9 (H in. H2O, V ft/min); 731.0 (lbf/in2); 481.3 (ft H2O); 265.7 (m H2O, V m/min); 31.0 (mm Hg); 84.9 (kPa). [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.2]
- Example 1: pond-side manometer 84.0 in. water, tap 4 in. above jet centreline gives H = 88 in., Vj = 138.9 x sqrt(88) = 1303 ft/min. [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.2]
- Note 2: V is at the vena contracta (see TIP 0410-02, -03, -04). [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.3]
- Note 4: headbox makers often supply their own head/velocity relation including elevation, tap velocity and headloss. [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.3]
- Note 5: edge flow adjustment at the tube bank changes velocity at pond-side transmitters and "will show an apparent change in total head which is not actually occurring in the main part of the jet". [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.3]
- Eq (5): "Q = Vj x Aj = Vj x Cc x b x w = K Cc b w sqrt(H)". [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.3]
- Example 2: 200 in. wide headbox, 0.75 in. slice, Cc 0.77, Vj 21.72 ft/s gives Q = 17.42 ft3/s = 7818 gpm (1 ft3/s = 448.83 gpm). [DOC a58b3808-c123-425c-8a4d-636b917678f9 p.3]
