## Operative values (verbatim)
- "Compressive strength is the single most important requirement for corrugated boxes, related to the edgewise compressive strength (ECT)" [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.5]
- Virgin kraft liner: "filler fibers can be used, up to about 15-20%"; "Up to 20% wastepaper use still permits the linerboard to be classified as virgin liner board in the USA" [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.8]
- White top liner: two separate fiber prep systems; "backwater from the white prep may feed to the brown prep, but not vice versa" [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.9]
- Starch "can be >5% of the final sheet weight, excluding starch used as a corrugating adhesive" [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.18]
- Newsprint from ONP: chemical pulp up to 30% by weight of furnish; ash 3-12 wt%; ink 1-2 wt% [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.26]
- Magazine stock: inorganic 10 wt% (uncoated) to 50 wt% (coated both sides); ink 1-7 wt% [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.27]
- Tissue: grammage as low as 5 g/m2 (copy paper about 75 g/m2) [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.33]
- Tissue brightness: premium minimum 75-80 ISO; industrial toilet tissue minimum 60-70 ISO; "Low ash is required to protect the creping blade" [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.43]
- Rejects and sludge table (total waste % dry weight): graphic news/magazines 15-20; graphic superior 10-25; hygienic 28-40; market DIP office paper 32-40; liner/fluting 4-9; board 4-9. Tables repeat on p.44 and p.61 [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.44]
- White ledger (US purchasing guideline, Feb 1999 No. 11): groundwood under 1 %, lignin under 3%, pH above 6.5, 90 g/m2, bursting 21.75 psi, tear 340 mN, thickness 0.105 mm, brightness above 81 ISO, at most 1 speck under .025 mm2 per 650 cm2 [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.60]
