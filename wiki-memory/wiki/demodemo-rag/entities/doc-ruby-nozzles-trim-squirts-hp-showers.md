## Operative values (verbatim)
- "Edge trimming and sheet break frequency are directly related." [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.2]
- "For fast machines a pressure of 400 to 500 psi (around 30 bar) is required to obtain a high jet speed, and by this a high kinetic jet energy despite a small jet diameter." [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.10]
- "Double jet nozzles are required when running higher machine speeds." [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.2]
- Tail cutter overhead under pickup felt: "a single jet nozzle with a rather big diameter (.025" to .030") should be used, set to the lowest possible operating pressure: 200 psi or less, depending on speed and sheet weight." [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.9]
- Tail cutter on the forming fabric: same nozzle type, pressure and geometry as the drive-side trim squirt [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.9]
- Operating pressure "must be set according to the needs: sheet weight, type of paper, speed" [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.8]
- "Be sure to use a portable stroboscope whenever you inspect or set nozzles." [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.11]
- Trim water feed: dual pre-filtration, double wall tank with level sensors, temperature control, ceramic 3-plunger pumps with pulsation damping, frequency-converter drives, automatic pressure control [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.16]
- HP shower oscillation: V_osc = PM speed (m/min) x Jet diameter (mm) / (Cloth length (m) x 60) = mm/sec [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.20]
- Needle jet water consumption table (litres/min vs jet diameter 0.30-1.20 mm and 5-40 bar): see [[hp-shower-nozzles-and-oscillation]] [DOC 9e4de3b6-86e4-4f21-98a8-d0cd74271432 p.20]
