## Operative values (quoted)
- "Ideal: about 20 mm long, along the grain, 4 to 5 mm thick. Acceptable prime range: 10 to 30 mm long, 3 to 6 mm thick." [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.13]
- Pin chips: normal length, under 3 mm wide and thick; fines pass 3 mm holes [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.18]
- Outside chip pile degradation: "about 1% of wood substance per month" [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.17]
- Chemical pulping is about 75% of North American production and about 97% of that is kraft [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.21]
- Yield classes: mechanical 85-95%, hybrid 55-85%, chemical 40-55% [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.21]
- Rule of thumb: pulps with yield >= 85% are mechanical [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.25]
- Table 4-4 kraft: NaOH + Na2S, 2-4 h, pH 13+, 170-180 C [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.23]
- Table 4-4 NSSC: Na2SO3 + Na2CO3, 1/4-1 h, pH 7-9, 160-180 C [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.23]
- Fig 4-8 world flows (million t/yr): virgin pulp 161 (market 33, captive 128), secondary fibre 74, fillers and coatings 10, paper and paperboard 238 [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.27]
- Pulp is usually dried to 80-90% oven dry for shipment [DOC cb6a70a2-a00a-4244-809b-d0effdcde3f1 p.29]
