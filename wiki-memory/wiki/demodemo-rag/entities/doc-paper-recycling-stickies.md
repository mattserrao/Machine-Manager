## Operative values (verbatim)
- Hot melts: "Soften at 150°F to 250°F"; "Density 0.9 to 1.0 g/cc" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.6]
- PSA: "Tacky at room temperature"; "Density 0.9 to 1.1 g/cc"; "Often (but not always) precipitate with drops in pH" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.8]
- "6 cut = 0.006 inches (0.15 mm) (150 micrometers)"; macrostickies are retained on a 6-cut slotted screen, microstickies pass it; "Trend is towards 0.10 mm or 0.075 mm slotted" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.10]
- Clarifier: "Filtrate to Clarifier: 2000 ppm suspended solids"; "Clarified water: 100 ppm suspended solids"; "Sludge: 3-7% solids"; "No change in colloidal or dissolved species" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.33]
- Dispersion: stock dewatered to 30%, steam heating screw to "185-245 C" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.41]
- Dispersion is "Not recommended, energy intensive and harm to the fibers" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.42]
- Screening principles: "Do not mix a clean stream with a dirty stream." "Avoid recirculation of contaminants." [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.26]
- Method effort (hours): bleaching and dyeing 4; deposition gravimetric 2, image analysis 3; Tappi T277 1.5; Port Townsend 1 1.5; Port Townsend 2 2 [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.59]
- Manual method: screen 100 g stock through 6-cut (0.150 mm) screen, count sticky spots per 100 g pulp [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.62]
- US Patent 7674355 (Pulmac macro and micro method) [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.67]
