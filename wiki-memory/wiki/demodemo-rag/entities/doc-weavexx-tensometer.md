## Operative values (verbatim)
- Principle: deflection of a flexible membrane under load depends on tensile stress; internal spring load, dial indicator in thousandths; chart converts to PLI [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.2]
- "The range of tension measurable with the instrument is 10 to 50 pounds per linear inch. Accuracy is +/- 10 percent of the converted tension reading." [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.2]
- Readings independent of speed or moisture; use on web at any angle, either side; dial face always toward same side [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.2]
- Steps: zero pointer with push button; hold by outer housing, "DO NOT contact the picture frame"; long side of frame in machine direction; press slowly until pointer stops (about 4 pounds hand pressure); read dial and convert; reset and measure "at least once more"; keep web running over frame in same direction [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.3]
- Safety: "do not apply the instrument to a moving web in the vicinity of an ingoing nip"; safer about 2 ft minimum from roll; not on top of a roll [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.3, p.5, p.6]
- Slack spans: measure close to a support roll (about 1 ft) to avoid wrinkling; time lapped seams out of the measurement [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.3, p.7]
- Chart p.9: axes tension 20-70 PLI vs dial reading 70-120; curves for "X" pick double layer; standard double layer / low caliper triple / fine single; standard triple / pulp double; Titan CDS, Titan SLX [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.9]
- Do not disassemble; clean externally and dry after each use [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.3]
