## Operative values (quoted)
- "Strong black liquor leaves at about 50-60% solids and goes to storage." For firing it "must be concentrated to 65-70% solids or higher" [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.2]
- Primary air ports supply "50 - 65% of the air requirement" [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.6]
- "after 2 to 6 months of operation a recovery boiler will generally plug on the fireside" and needs a waterwash [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.10]
- Boiler outlet gas minimum about 130 C; each 15 C rise costs about 1% thermal efficiency; 2% drop in liquor concentration costs about 1% [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.13]
- Kiln: slope 1/4 to 1/2 inch per foot, 0.5 to 2.0 rpm, retention 2 to 4 hours [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.21]
- Brightness: unbleached kraft 15-30, fully bleached sulfite about 94 Elrepho [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.28]
