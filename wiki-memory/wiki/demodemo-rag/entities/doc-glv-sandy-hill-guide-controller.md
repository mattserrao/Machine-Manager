## Operative values
- "precision two-way pneumatic control device" regulating airflow to each guide air diaphragm, pushing the actuator housing holding the end of the guide roll back and forth [DOC 5e262f9c-c4c3-43d7-9951-7107e3ff6b83 p.1]
- "The fabric edge runs in contact with a flat paddle projecting from the controller, called a palm"; palm held by a pivot arm and return spring, motion passes through the spring to the needle valve [DOC 5e262f9c-c4c3-43d7-9951-7107e3ff6b83 p.1]
- Needle valve has three positions: I balanced (equal pressure in both diaphragms, slight constant bleed so needles do not take a permanent set), II and III pressure one diaphragm and exhaust the other when the fabric runs off in the cross-machine direction; valve "will not jump in steps but will move evenly" [DOC 5e262f9c-c4c3-43d7-9951-7107e3ff6b83 p.1] [DOC 5e262f9c-c4c3-43d7-9951-7107e3ff6b83 p.2]
- "The controller is precalibrated for proper operation in a normal guiding application. Refer to 5.4.2 for the correct procedure for recalibrating" (5.4.2 not in this excerpt) [DOC 5e262f9c-c4c3-43d7-9951-7107e3ff6b83 p.2]
- Fittings on the drawing: .25 NPT inlet for main supply air, .25 NPT outlets to guide air diaphragms, optional rubber protective boot for wet-end applications [DOC 5e262f9c-c4c3-43d7-9951-7107e3ff6b83 p.2]
- Checklist sections: auto guides (9 checks), hand guides (5), controllers/palm valves (8), other (air lines, filters/regulators/gauges, roll surfaces); columns OK / needs maintenance / needs replacement [DOC 5e262f9c-c4c3-43d7-9951-7107e3ff6b83 p.3]
