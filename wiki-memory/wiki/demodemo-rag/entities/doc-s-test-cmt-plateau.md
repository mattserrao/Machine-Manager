## Operative values (verbatim)
- CMT: "10 pieces of paper with the dimensions of 12.7 mm x 150 mm (1/2 x 6 inches) are cut in the MD direction ... fluted in the laboratory fluter set to 177 C (+-8 C)"; crushed immediately (CMT0) or "after 30 minutes conditioning in 23 C and 50% RH (for the CMT30 value)" [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.2]
- "the CMT0 is higher than the CMT30" (moisture difference) [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.2]
- Pre-crush table: 9% pre-crush: thickness loss 0%, FCT loss 2%, Flat Crush Hardness loss 16%; 35% pre-crush: thickness loss 5%, FCT loss 7%, hardness loss 100% [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.4]
- Starch at size press, recycled 90 g/m2: "6-7 N per % starch" on CMT plateau vs "15 N per %" on CMTmax [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.8]
- Standard SCT span "changed from 0.7 mm to 4 mm"; SCT-long showed no useful correlation (non-reproducible buckling); "an offset of 1 mm in the 4 mm span was introduced" giving the S shape [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.8] [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.9]
- A flute height about 4.5 mm (0.18 in); B flute about 2.5 mm (0.1 in), proposed for 90 g/m2 (18#) and below [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.7]
- Correlation set: April 2015, 10 grades from 17 European mills = 170 samples; 120 more samples 6 months later confirmed it [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.9] [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.10]
- "Since May 2015 an S-test measuring unit is mounted in L&W Autoline" at Smurfit Kappa Hoya (Germany); "correlation coefficient of 97.6% between the measured S-test and the real CMTplateau value" [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.11]
