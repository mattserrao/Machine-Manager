## Operative values
- Batch starch cooking 88-93 C held 20-30 min [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.1]
- Size press: starch about 4-10% solids, pickup 70-110 lb per ton; calender stack pickup about 10 lb per ton [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.1]
- Coating: pigments 70-85% by weight, binder 15-30%, each additive under 1% [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.2]
- Coating colour total solids about 50% to over 70% [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.2]
- Coating 25-35% of total sheet weight; kitchen supplies about 200-400 tonnes/day for a 600-1100 tonnes/day machine [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.4]
- "For the highest-quality printing papers, supercalendering has no satisfactory substitute." [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.11]
- About 25% of supercalender drive power goes into roll heating [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.11]
- Off-machine supercalender speed generally limited to about 750 m/min [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.12]
- Drying shrinks the coating by about 45 to 70 % [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.10]
- ISO paper/board dividing line 224 g/m2 (about 0.010 inch) [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.13]
- Web consistency leaving each multiply forming mold cannot go much above about 15% without hurting plybond [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.19]
