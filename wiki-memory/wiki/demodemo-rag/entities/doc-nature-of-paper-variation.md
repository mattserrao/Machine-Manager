## Operative values (verbatim)
- "The range S +/- S contains 67% of all the data, and +/- 2s contains 95%"; process control vendors "have chosen 2s as a unit of variation". [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.1]
- Reducing variation lets the target be lowered at the same break frequency, "the primary justification for process control". [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.1]
- Breaks occur where stress-carrying ability falls below imposed stress; reduce by higher strength target, less strength variation, lower average stress, less stress variation. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.2]
- Beta gauge response about 1 s: at 700 m/min it "could not record wavelengths shorter than 11.7 m (38 ft)". [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.3]
- Four directions: MD, CD, random or residual (R), vertical or thickness (ZD). [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.3]
- MD bands: long term longer than 200 s; medium 200 to 1 s; short term 1 s to 100 mm; formation smaller than 100 mm. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.3]
- Table I (average of eight records, share of total variance): 25-250 Hz 15%; 2.5-25 Hz 40%; 0.25-2.5 Hz 10%; 0.006-0.25 Hz 20%; 0.0016-0.006 Hz 15%. At 700 m/min, 25 Hz = 0.47 m and 2.5 Hz = 4.7 m. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.3-4]
- Any statement of variation "must include a statement regarding the largest and smallest wavelengths included". [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.4]
- Traversing on-machine gauges do not give a true CD profile (signal contains MD and R); best true CD samples are cut from the reel. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.4]
- CD profile range "leveled out at about 10 plies"; standardised on 10 plies; CD profiles still change reel to reel. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.6]
- Residual R is determined by subtraction; R keeps climbing to 25 plies, so number of plies needed is unknown. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.7]
- Data box size changes COV: 13 mm 2.15%; 25 mm 2.12%; 50 mm 2.04%; 100 mm 1.86%; 150 mm 1.77%; early digital systems used 150 mm (6 in.) boxes. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.7, p.9]
- Some CD control drove COV "to less than 0.1%, and the paper was still causing quality problems"; a "streak" index was needed. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.7]
- ZD: twin-wire sheets tend to be fines lean in the centre; case hardening example on two corrugating medium machines fixed by "Reducing press load and installing a softer nip". [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.8]
- Temperature-gradient calendering gives higher gloss at the same bulk; moisture gradient had little effect; steam shower on calender stack improves smoothness on the steamed side. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.9-10]
