## Operative values
- "Most papermaking fibers have a weight-averaged DP in the 600-1500 range." [DOC e80d6cb4-2ca6-4bbd-8748-ea1f68624dad p.19]
- Softwood fibre 4.0 mm x 40 um (ratio 100); hardwood 2.0 mm x 22 um (ratio 90) [DOC e80d6cb4-2ca6-4bbd-8748-ea1f68624dad p.19]
- "wood provides about 93% of the world's virgin fiber requirement"; about 40% of paper products are recycled [DOC e80d6cb4-2ca6-4bbd-8748-ea1f68624dad p.24]
- World demand for pulp and paper expected to grow about 3% per year [DOC e80d6cb4-2ca6-4bbd-8748-ea1f68624dad p.18]
- Softwood avg composition: cellulose 42+-2%, hemicelluloses 27+-2%, lignin 28+-3%, extractives 3+-2%; hardwood cellulose 45+-2%, matrix polysaccharides 30+-5%, lignin 20+-4%, extractives 5+-3% [DOC e80d6cb4-2ca6-4bbd-8748-ea1f68624dad p.30]
- S2 layer holds 80-90% of fibre-wall material and governs mechanical properties [DOC e80d6cb4-2ca6-4bbd-8748-ea1f68624dad p.26]
- Wood relative density: 0.31 (western red cedar) to 0.55 (western larch) for softwoods [DOC e80d6cb4-2ca6-4bbd-8748-ea1f68624dad p.28]
- Other tables (Table 2-2, Table 2-3 pulpwood length/diameter/density) are summarised on [[wood-and-fibre-properties]] [DOC e80d6cb4-2ca6-4bbd-8748-ea1f68624dad p.29]
