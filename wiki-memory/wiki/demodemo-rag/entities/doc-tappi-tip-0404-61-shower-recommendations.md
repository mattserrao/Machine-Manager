## Operative values (verbatim)
- Needle jets: optimum placement "from 6 to 10 inches (150 to 250 mm)" from the outside of the fabric; best cleaning in zone two and early zone three. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.2]
- "needle showers should not be run at a pressure higher than absolutely necessary"; full-width showers usually no higher than about 600 psi (4,140 kPa). [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.3]
- "it is absolutely imperative that needle jet showers be oscillated"; stroke length an integer multiple of nozzle spacing (2 to 4x common); optimal rate one nozzle width per fabric revolution; most common needle orifice .040 in (1 mm); oscillator reversal time near zero. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.3]
- Running Void Volume: RVV = C x W x S x V / 19.25 (gpm; C caliper in, W width in, S speed fpm, V void factor "use 0.6"). [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.5]
- Flooded nip shower water clarity "100 ppm or better of suspended solids"; good cleaning seen with volumes as low as 20% of full RVV (not for knock-off). [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.5]
- Standard oscillator stroke 12 in (300 mm); nozzle spacing 6 in typical, 3 or 4 in because 12 is an even multiple. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.6]
- Table 1 forming (psi, gpm/in): headbox 20-40, 0.075-0.0204 [sic]; breast roll 30-40, 0.167-0.197; apron 30-40; dandy roll needle 100-150; couch roll needle 350-500, 0.212-0.250; flooded nip 200-350, 0.75-1.77; sheet knock-off 200-350; inside / sheet side HP 200-450, 0.167-0.238; wash roll 40-50; grooved roll 150-350; flat box lube 20-40, .07-.09. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.8]
- Table 2 press: flooding 40-60 psi, nozzles 3 in; inside HP 200-350; outside HPS 150-300 at 6-8 in; uhle box lube 20-30 psi, 0.07-0.09 gpm/in; doctor lube 25-40; suction roll 350-500, 0.21-0.25; grooved roll <1400 fpm fan 60-80, >1400 fpm needle 200-500; traversing 150-300. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.9]
- Outside high-pressure shower is "probably the most important shower in the cleaning system", continuous, angled up to 15 degrees into the run. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.10]
- Grooved roll shower: fan on machines under 1400 fpm (430 m/min), oscillating needle above. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.10]
- Trim squirt nozzles run above 600 psi (4,000 kPa). [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.11]
- Water clarity (100 mesh strained): 0-50 ppm = filtered fresh; 50-75 for 0.040 in orifices; 75-100 for 0.055 in; 100-200 for 0.125 in; 200-500 brush showers; 500+ purgeable. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.11]
- Hardness over 100 ppm CaCO3 can plug nozzles (inverse solubility); mild acid cleaner in production, stronger at shutdown. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.12]
- Maintenance: daily inspect oscillators and fan nozzles; daily check lube shower alignment; actuate brush showers once per shift; keep pressure gauges working. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.12]
- Metric Table 2a: outside HPS 1,000-2,100 kPa at 150-200 mm; suction roll 2,400-3,500 kPa; uhle lube 140-200 kPa. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.15]
