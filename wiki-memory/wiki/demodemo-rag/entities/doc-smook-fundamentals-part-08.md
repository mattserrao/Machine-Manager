## Operative values
- Gap between plate faces typically 0.1 mm to 1.0 mm [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.3]
- Disc refiner peripheral speed about 4700 to 5700 fpm; no-load power rises with the cube of rotor speed [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.3]
- Components metered into the machine chest at 2.8 to 3.2% consistency; machine chest held 2.6 to 2.8%; headbox about 1% or less [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.7]
- Screen slots: 0.008" (early 1990s) then 0.006" then 0.004" [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.13]
- Flotation: ink above about 50 um; 6-10 cells in series; clay 8-10% minimum, 12-14% preferred [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.19]
- Alkaline papermaking pH 7.0-8.0; calcium carbonate buffers at pH 7.2-8.0 [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.29]
