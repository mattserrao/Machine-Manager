## Operative values (verbatim)
- "The most effective liquid ring vacuum pump performance test that can be performed in the paper mill is the flat plate orifice test method", done with the pump isolated from the machine. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.1]
- Boroscope inspection of rotor and cone gives a rougher, "less accurate" capacity estimate. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.1]
- "An iron rotor-cone pump can easily lose 30% to 50% of original capacity over ten to twenty years"; stainless "may lose only 5% to 10%" over ten years. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.2]
- Maximum stable vacuum: read two analog vacuum gauges per pump end; if the needle flutters open the regulating valve slowly until still; if no more vacuum, too much water, back off until it just flutters, then increase slightly. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.2]
- Seal water control: no device = too much water; on-off ball valve = no variable control; single orifice disc = one nominal flow; linear regulating valve lets flow be reset as clearance opens. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.3]
- % original capacity = tested capacity / manufacturer curve capacity at same rpm and vacuum; example 5,950 m3/hr (3,500 ACFM) design at 380 mm Hg (15 inHg) tested 5,100 m3/hr (3,000 ACFM) = 86%. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.3]
- Equation 1: "$Ea = kW x $UR x hr x day" (or BHP x 0.746); Equation 2: "$SWa = l/min x 60 x $SWr x hr x day". [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.3]
- Wasted cost: 86% pump at 215 kW (160 BHP) wastes 14% = 30.1 kW (22.4 BHP); 227 l/min (60 gpm) seal water wastes 32 l/min (4 gpm). [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.3]
- Equation 3: "$Ewa = $Ea x (1-%OC)"; Equation 4: "$SWwa = $SWa x (1-%OC)". [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.4]
- Worn and new pumps draw the same horsepower; for extreme clearance, +100% seal water pushed water out the discharge and raised power 10%. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.4]
