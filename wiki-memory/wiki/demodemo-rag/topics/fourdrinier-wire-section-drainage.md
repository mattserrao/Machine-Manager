## Layout (Fig 16-27)
- Fabric runs from breast roll to couch roll; forming board follows the breast roll, then table rolls/foils, wet boxes, dry boxes (flatboxes), dandy roll, suction couch. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.12] [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.13]
- Couch roll is a hollow perforated shell with one or two stationary high-vacuum suction boxes; most of the wire drive is on the couch and wire turning roll. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.12]
- Edge strips are cut before the couch with high-pressure water jets and go to the couch pit and broke. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.12]
## Forming board, table rolls, foils
- Forming board slows initial drainage so fines and fillers are not washed out; lead blade fixes jet angle. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.13]
- Table rolls give a vacuum pulse in the diverging gap and a pressure pulse at the entering nip; strong pulses at high speed disrupt formation and fines retention. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.13]
- Foils: fixed surface, divergence angles about 0.5-3.0 degrees (larger = more vacuum), 3 to 10 blades per assembly, HDPE or ceramic. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.13]
- Foils give lower nip vacuum that lasts longer; closer spacing gives much more drainage. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.14]
- Low-head vacuum foil: text says up to 20 in water vacuum, caption says up to 10 in (CONFLICT within the page). [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.14]
## Flatboxes
- Vacuum must rise along the row; Fig 16-37 boxes 1-7: water removed about 12 to 1.5-2 lb/lb fibre, vacuum about 2 to 6-6.5 in. Hg. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.15]
## Fabric
- Single layer cheapest for intermediate speeds; double and triple layers replaced them on faster machines; functions: drainage, sheet support (support points per area), mechanical stability. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.15]
- "Drain as much as possible using static and low-vacuum elements before using high vacuum." [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.16]
- Consistency limits for static elements: slow newsprint about 2.0%, high-caliper food board about 1.5-1.7%, free-draining fine paper above 3%. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.16]
## Agitation and zones
- Sheraton roll (sawtooth table roll) worked well when driven at the resonant frequency of the free wire span. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.17]
- Dandy roll overspeed up to 5% improved formation on some machines; modern placement where consistency is about 2%, 35-40 mesh surface. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.17] [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.18]
- Four drainage zones: blend, formation, transition, high pressure difference. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.17]
- Dry line near the second dry box is read as a wet-end indicator; see bleed panels in [[headbox-types-and-pulsation]]. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.8]
