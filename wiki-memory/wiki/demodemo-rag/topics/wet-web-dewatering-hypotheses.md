## Hypotheses (Table 2)
- 1 General: changes in relative positions of solids, including density vs position, "can play a major role with respect to dewatering rates" [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.4]
- 2 Densification of surface layers adjacent to where water leaves can dominate, depending on fiber conformability [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.4]
- 3 Plugging by fines, especially at high basis weight and fines content [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.4]
- 4 Sealing: fabric sealing and mutual-conformability sealing between fibers; well-refined kraft fibers [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.4]
- 5 Flocculation unfavorable to vacuum and wet-press dewatering, worse with retention aids [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.5]
- 6 Healing: at low consistency, no flocculants, fibers fill thin areas within about 2 mm or less [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.5]
- 7 Rewetting by film splitting, meniscus interfacial tension, capillary forces during re-expansion [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.5]
- Fig. 4 adds a sixth phenomenon, thin membranes of fine matter facing the press felt [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.15-16]

## Consistency and forces (Table 3)
- Jet impingement 0.15 to 1.2%: inertia, shear; forming board 0.15 to 1.2%: delay of filtration [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.5]
- Hydrofoils 0.4 to 4%: pressure pulse, main suction pulse; vacuum boxes 2 to 18%: doctoring, suction compression and filtration, rewetting [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.5]
- Vacuum couch roll 14 to 20%; wet-press 16 to 55%: pressure filtration, then expansion, rewetting [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.5]
- Fig. 5 water:solids ratios along the machine: 70 to 700, 25 to 50, 6 to 10, 4 to 6, 0.8 to 2 (breast roll through press nips) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.16]

## Definitions
- Filtration dominates between the forming board and the dry line; after the dry line, displacement dewatering (air displaces water) and compression dewatering (volume decreases under pressure) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.5-6]
- Cost trend: water removal becomes "increasingly expensive, on a mass basis, as the process proceeds" [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.2]
- Higher solids leaving the press raise speed on drier-limited machines and improve press runnability [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.2]
