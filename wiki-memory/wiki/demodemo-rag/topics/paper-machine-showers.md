## Principles
- Fan showers flood contaminants away (even CD volume matters most); needle jets apply power: "should not be run at a pressure higher than absolutely necessary". [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.1, p.3]
- Needle jet standoff "6 to 10 inches (150 to 250 mm)"; closer placement wastes pressure energy; reduce pressure rather than move closer. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.2-3]
- Oscillate needle jets; stroke = integer multiple of nozzle spacing; 12 in stroke with 6/4/3 in spacing. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.3, p.6]

## Press section (Table 2)
- Outside HP: 150-300 psi (1,000-2,100 kPa), 6 in spacing, 6-8 in from fabric, continuous. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.9-10]
- Uhle box lube: 20-30 psi, 6-8 in spacing, 0.07-0.09 gpm/in, flow lands 1-2 in before the box cover. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.9-10]
- Suction roll cleaning: 350-500 psi, 3 in spacing, 4 in away. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.10]
- Grooved roll: fan 60-80 psi below 1400 fpm (430 m/min); needle 200-500 psi above. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.10]

## Forming section (Table 1)
- Couch roll needle 350-500 psi; flooded nip 200-350 psi; inside and sheet-side HP 200-450 psi; wash roll 40-50 psi. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.8]
- Sheet side HP most effective 6-8 in from the fabric, angled 15 degrees into the run. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.6]

## Water and upkeep
- Clarity: 0-50 ppm like filtered fresh; 0.040 in nozzles need 50-75 ppm or better; 500+ ppm purgeable showers. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.11]
- Hardness over 100 ppm CaCO3 plugs nozzles; daily oscillator and nozzle checks. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.12]
- Trim squirts above 600 psi (4,000 kPa) for heavy grades. [DOC a1b709c1-b90f-4687-ad80-b9fb21515901 p.11] Compare Heimbach 15-40 bar in [[edge-trim-showers]].
