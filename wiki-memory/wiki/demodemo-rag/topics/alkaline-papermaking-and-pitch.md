## Pitch
- Deposits on forming fabric (holes), press felts (lower permeability) and rolls (pickouts, uneven peeling); removable only by solvent scrubbing or special cleaners. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.29]
- Pitch is oily low-molecular-weight material (fatty acids, resin acids, esters) precipitated as calcium and magnesium salts; the concern is "depositable pitch", not total pitch. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.29]
- Acid systems: alum fixes pitch to fibers. Alkaline systems: nonionic wetting agents, reduce hardness, chelating agents; add chemicals before agglomeration starts. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.29]

## Alkaline (neutral) papermaking
- European fine paper mills converted in the 1970s-80s; drivers: brightness and opacity demand, cheaper calcium carbonate. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.29]
- Benefits: stronger sheet, more filler (ash 18-25% common), filler replaces costly fiber, less corrosion, easier system closure. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.29]
- Calcium carbonate buffers the system at pH 7.2-8.0; "alkaline" systems actually run at pH 7.0-8.0, so "neutral papermaking" is preferred by many. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.29]
- Ground calcium carbonate (wet-ground chalk) and precipitated calcium carbonate (flue gas through lime milk) can be used alone or blended. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.29]
- Synthetic reactive sizes (AKD, ASA) are central; see [[wet-end-additives-sizing-fillers-dyes]]. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.29]
