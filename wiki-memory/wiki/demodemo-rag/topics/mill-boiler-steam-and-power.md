## Water supply (25.1) and feedwater (25.2)
- Water treatment covers sedimentation, filtration (downflow pressure filter, Fig. 25-1; Table 25-2 media layers), solids-contact clarifier (Fig. 25-2), coagulation, flocculation, chlorination, ozonation, lime softening, ion-exchange softening [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.21]
- Feedwater treatment goals: prevent deposits, reduce corrosion, prevent boiler water carryover; covers pH control, phosphate and chelant treatment, deionization for high-pressure boilers, oxygen scavenging, neutralizing amines, defoamers [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.22]

## Boilers and fuels (25.3)
- Fuels: coal, bark, hog fuel, oil, spent liquor, gas; circulating fluidized combustion (CFC) uses sand or limestone to burn low-grade or high-moisture fuels [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.23]
- Heat release per pound of combustible: carbon to CO 4,400 BTU; carbon to CO2 14,600 BTU; hydrogen to water 62,000 BTU [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.24]
- Boiler thermal efficiency 65 to 90% [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.25]
- Exit gas temperature is limited by acid condensation; about 5 to 10% of process air enters the furnace through infiltration [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.26]
- Hog fuel moisture is the main problem; Fig. 25-8 plots steam generation vs hog fuel moisture; drying methods covered (Figs. 25-9 to 25-11) [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.27] [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.29]

## Steam utilization (25.4) and power (25.5)
- About half of process steam is used indirectly, so condensate return saves heat and water treatment cost [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.28]
- Cogeneration extracts steam at intermediate and low pressure; condensing vs back-pressure generation compared [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.28]
- Fig. 25-14 energy balance: about 39% output power for condensing and 89% for power plus process heat with by-product generation [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.30]
- Where condensate contamination is possible, monitoring is needed on condensate-return systems [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.29]
- Electrical: 115 kV supply, 13.2 kV distribution, 4,160 V and 550 V motors; purchased power charges depend on peak power, load factor, power factor [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.30]
- Energy management (25.6) starts at book p.377: see [[mill-energy-management]]
