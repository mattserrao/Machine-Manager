Source: [[doc-weavexx-tensometer]] (DOC d08922bf). Related: [[forming-fabric-guiding]], [[press-felt-start-up]], [[press-felt-construction-and-conditioning]].

- Instrument range 10-50 PLI, accuracy +/-10% of converted reading; dial in thousandths converted by chart [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.2]
- Procedure: zero, hold by housing, long frame side in MD, press to about 4 lb hand pressure, convert, repeat at least once [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.3]
- Stay away from ingoing nips; not on a roll top; measure near a support on slack spans [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.3, p.6, p.7]
- Chart curve depends on fabric construction (double/triple layer, Titan CDS/SLX); the curves are not readable in the extracted text, so PLI values per dial reading are not in the wiki [DOC d08922bf-54ab-4869-b4c6-b7c0d251f22a p.9]
- Why it matters: guiding needs correct tension, checked on both sides at the same position [DOC 59151609-8cbd-461a-874e-aaeb006dfc27 p.2]; Voith says maintain tension during felt start-up and check frequently [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.3]
