## Definitions
- "% Overall Retention = amount retained in sheet / amount added with stock"; "% Single-Pass Retention = amount retained in sheet / amount from headbox". [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.22]
- Overall retention drives additive cost (lost with white water overflow); single-pass retention drives quality and machine operation. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.23]
- Low single-pass retention means high white-water recycle, uneven distribution, two-sidedness on fourdrinier sheets, fines accumulation retarding drainage, more pitch and slime build-up. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.23]
- Particles are normally negatively charged, so many additives are made cationic. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.22]
- Mechanisms: filtration, chemical bonding, colloidal phenomena, adsorption; only about 2% of 0.2 um TiO2 pigment is retained by filtration. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.23]
- Table 15-2 factors: stock (pH, consistency, temperature, fiber, closure), on the wire (grammage, formation, fabric, dewatering elements, speed, shake), additives (fillers, order of addition, ionic balance, anionic trash). [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.23]

## Wet-end chemistry and zeta potential
- Overuse of cationic polymers flocculates the fibers themselves and formation suffers. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.24]
- Wastepaper furnishes carry more solubles and hurt controllability of the wet end. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.24]
- "The best retention of fine particles and colloids ... normally occurs when the zeta potential is near zero." Polyvalent cations Al+++ and Fe+++ are most effective. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.24]
- Alum: Al2(SO4)3 + 6H2O → 3H2SO4 + 2Al(OH)3; flocculates by bridging but is shear sensitive and less effective at higher machine speeds. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.24]
- Synthetic polyelectrolytes (cationic or anionic) resist shear, are less pH dependent, used very dilute. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.24]
- CONFLICT in the literature as reported: optimum zeta near zero (Fig 15-3) vs close to -9 mV; commercial practice has not always confirmed either. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.25]
- Proper role of on-line zeta: characterize a well-operated system and flag deviations. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.25]

## Retention aids
- Added to thin stock before the headbox; headbox dispersion must be adequate to avoid overflocculated stock. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.23]
- Polyacrylamides (cationic and anionic) are over 80% of conventional retention aid formulations; mechanisms bridging, patch, charge neutralization. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.27]
- Dual polymer: low molecular weight cationic polymer first, then high molecular weight anionic polymer. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.28]
- Microparticle systems (1980s): colloidal silica or modified bentonite with a high molecular weight polymer; over 300 machines use first-generation systems, mostly fine paper or virgin board; allow higher filler loading. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.28]
