## Dissolved oxygen
- Natural water at 20 C holds about 9 ppm DO [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.6]
- Table 26-2 DO (ppm) fresh / saline (5 g/L chloride): 0 C 14.6/13.8; 5 C 12.8/12.1; 10 C 11.3/10.7; 15 C 10.2/9.7; 20 C 9.2/8.7; 25 C 8.4/8.0; 30 C 7.6/7.3 [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.6]
- Simple soluble compounds (sugars) break down quickly; lignin derivatives and tannins slowly; full oxidation can take up to 100 days [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.6]
- DO electrodes should be checked regularly against the Winkler titration [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.6]
- Pulp and paper pollution categories: effluent solids, oxygen demand, toxicity, colour; abatement mainly targets solids and oxygen demand [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.6]

## BOD5 and other oxygen tests
- BOD5: sample incubated at 20 C for five days; effluent diluted so depletion stays near 7 ppm; about 60-70% of total oxidation occurs in the first five days [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.6]
- Table 26-4 tests: BOD5, IOD (immediate oxygen demand: SO2, sulfides, hydrosulfites), COD (dichromate or permanganate), TOD, TOC [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.7]
- BOD takes five days so gives no quick control feedback; no inherent correlation with other tests (Fig. 26-1 TOC vs BOD5 scatter for seven bleached kraft mills; Fig. 26-2 TDS vs BOD5 for decker filtrate R = 0.876) [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.7] [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.8]
- Rapid on-line BOD analyzers (miniature bioreactor on plastic rings) report relatively high maintenance; the described unit holds the inflow-outflow DO difference at 3 ppm by adjusting dilution [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.7]

## Effluent solids (Table 26-3)
- TDS (evaporate filtrate), TSS (tared filter), total solids, settleable (usually one hour), floatable, nonsettleable (colloidal), volatile (burn off at 600 C), fixed (residue after 600 C) [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.7]
- Settleable organic solids are the most objectionable: sludge blankets deplete oxygen and turn anaerobic (methane, hydrogen sulfide) [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.6]

## Toxicity
- Known toxic constituents: resin acids, unsaturated fatty acids, chlorinated phenolics; fish bioassay survival usually measured after 96 hours; conventional tests miss sublethal and long-term effects [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.8]
- Environmental effects monitoring: stations downstream plus upstream control (Fig. 26-4); biological monitoring with artificial substrates for long-term effects [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.11]
