## Definition and basis
- Separation of a heterogeneous fiber mixture into two or more streams, at least one better suited to an end use. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.42]
- Basis: fiber length, diameter, flexibility, specific surface area, springwood/summerwood, hardwood/softwood, lignin (wettability), brightness/color, kappa. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.43]

## Hardware
- Pressure screens (length, flexibility, fines), washers, gravity screens, deckers, high-speed belt washers, centrifugal cleaners; rarely froth flotation, spinning disc, dry sorting. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.44]
- Pressure screen: accepts carry fines, short and flexible fibers; reject carries long whole fibers. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.45]
- Pressure screen data (groundwood, second stage rejects): feed consistency 1.61, long 2.71, short 0.75; shives 10.9 / 16.19 / 2.45%; burst index 8.18 / 7.10 / 11.5; tear index 65.1 / 68.4 / 52.3. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.46]
- Gravity screen wash: feed 1% consistency, fines/ash/ink out at 0.35%, long fiber out at 4% with 75% yield. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.47]
- High-speed belt washer: feed 1%; fines, ink, ash at 0.35% consistency, 25% of feed solids, 90% of feed flow; washed stock 10-14% consistency. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.49]
- Centrifugal cleaner: accepts = overflow, rejects = underflow; overflow has lower consistency and freeness, flexible well-refined fibers, fines; underflow has higher consistency and freeness, coarse stiff unrefined fibers, summerwood. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.50]
- Automated dry sorting (color, lignin by optical means, air jets): "Technology is in its infancy, but is operating commercially"; manual sorting very common. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.54]

## Applications
- Goals: cheaper furnish from lower cost fiber, improved strength, unique sheets, reduced refining energy and screening/cleaning. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.55]
- Hot stock fractionation: unwashed brown stock pressure-screened into high-hardwood low-kappa top-liner stock and softwood-rich base stock; "Now common practice in virgin linerboard mills". [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.56]
- Pressure-screen shorts are usually clean enough for top liner without fine screening; the long fraction is harder to fine screen. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.57]
- Engineered three-layer sheet: short fibers outside (stiffness), long inside (bulk), stiffer than uniform sheet of equal weight. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.58]
- Cylinder board: 2 layers top liner, 4-6 layers filler, sometimes 2 layers back liner. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.59]
- Separate grades on separate machines (e.g. linerboard and medium from OCC) generally not feasible at one site. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.60]
- Selective refining: refine the long fraction, recombine with shorts to improve strength, save energy, cut fines; Fig. 19.5 burst vs CSF for European OCC (long:short 36:64). [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.62]

## The yield penalty
- Shorts fraction is low consistency and freeness: pressure screens shorts 1-2% and 150-300 CSF; centrifugal cleaners <0.5% and 20-300 CSF; flotation <1% high ash; washing <0.3% high ash. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.64]
- "Deink systems typically must have a yield of 70-85%"; OCC or mixed waste yields must be much higher; "No yield loss, no fractionation, no property improvement." [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.64]
- Greatest potential: multilayer papermaking from a common furnish, sludge upgrading, market pulp grades from mixed recycled paper, dry sorting. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.65]
- Conclusion: viable in selected cases, especially multi-layered boards. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.67]
