- Surface lignin is hard to remove (condensed or lignin-carbohydrate complexes): oxygen cuts total lignin about 50% but surface about 15%. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.12]
- Surface extractives far exceed bulk content; oxygen stage and peroxide reduce them, ozone and ClO2 do not. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.14-15]
- Carboxyl charge (uronic and hexenuronic acids) falls mainly where ozone or ClO2 destroy hexenuronic acid. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.19]
- More charge gives more swelling, flexibility, relative bonded area and tensile; hardwood responds more than softwood. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.23, p.27]
- Charged pulps develop bonding faster in beating, so less refining energy for a given bonding level. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.25, p.27]
- Water adhesion rises strongly after the oxygen stage. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.16]
