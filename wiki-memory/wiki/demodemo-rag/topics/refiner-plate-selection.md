## Amount of refining (specific energy)
- Net specific energy = (total applied power - no-load) / t/d; units hpd/t or kWh/t; throughput on oven dry basis. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.25]
- Typical net hpd/t (Table 4): fine paper HWD 2-5, SWD 3-7, tickler 1-1.5; linerboard base 5-7, top 10-12; news SWD kraft 2-5, TMP/GWD 1-5; GWD printing SWD 3-7, TMP/GWD 3-6. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.26]
- Freeness drop per net hpd/t (Table 5): bleached SWD 20-40 ml, bleached HWD 60-100, GWD 3-7, OCC 40-70, mixed office 50-70, news 20-35. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.26]
- Keep available power about 25% above the nominal expected level. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.26]

## Intensity (specific edge load)
- Table 6 (Ws/m): SWD kraft 1.0-2.5; HWD kraft 0.1-0.5; recycle 0.2-0.8; TMP/GWD 0.2-0.5. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.28]
- Hardwood: lower intensity gives more bulk and opacity at given strength and less energy per freeness drop (freeness drop per net hpd/t rises as intensity falls from 2.0 to 0.2 Ws/m). [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.27] [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.28]
- Mechanical post-refining: low intensity gives higher freeness, longer fibre, better tear at same debris. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.28]
- CONFLICT: hardwood range 0.1-0.5 Ws/m [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.28] vs "0.3-0.8 Ws/m" in the case study [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.42] vs mills at 0.6-1.0 [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.27].
- Lower SEL bound is set by minimum bar and groove widths for a given refiner size and speed; going lower needs more equipment. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.29]

## Bar and groove widths
- Bar width limited by metal contamination: over 0.050 in, about 0.075 in where baling wire is likely. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.29]
- Groove width limited by plugging: groundwood 0.050 in, hardwood at least 0.075 in, softwood at least 0.090 or 0.125 in. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.29]
- Reduced periphery plates trade diameter for lower no-load; see Table 7 savings in [[doc-aft-intro-stock-prep-refining]]. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.29]
