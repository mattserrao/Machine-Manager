## Construction
- Press felt supports and carries the web, helps dewater, and drives other press parts like a transmission belt [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.2]
- Required: durability, dimensional stability, permeability, cleanability, pliability, surface finish [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.2]
- Evolution: spun woven wool felts, then 1950s needled batt-on-base, then batt-on-mesh with synthetic yarns [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.2]
- Multilayer fabrics have pronounced knuckles that can mark some grades; planar bases (MD and CD yarns not interwoven) address this; multilayer fabrics need suction boxes before the nip for released water [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.3]
- Apertured (membrane) felts: perforated polymer membrane between base and batt; wears in faster and reaches peak performance sooner [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.3]
- Fabric selected jointly by supplier and papermaker, often after trials; depends on grade, basis weight, machine type, press severity, conditioning equipment [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.3]

## Conditioning
- "High- and low-pressure showers are the main tool for loosening and flushing out fines and fillers." [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.3]
- Parts of a conditioning system (Albany): detergent, flooding, lubricating and high-pressure showers, plus felt suction pipe; a suction box or wringer press removes loosened material; detergents or chemicals if water alone is not enough [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.3]
- Traversing HP shower: one nozzle on a drive chain and trolley; "Delivers 1.0 to 1.5 gpm of water at 1000 psig" (Lodding Engineering) [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.4]
- Forming-side brush can help dislodge contaminants [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.4]
- Uhle box slot width, vacuum factor and one-vs-two boxes per felt (Sweet): [[uhle-box-sizing]] [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]

## Sheet-control view of felt design and conditioning (AstenJohnson, see [[sheet-control-press-section]])
- Uhle box vacuum below about 16-18 in Hg (54-61 kPa) depending on batt fineness; active vacuum control above 18 in Hg (61 kPa) [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.19] [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.24]
- HP showers: fine paper / coated board 150-250 psi (10.3-17.2 bar); coarse-cap packaging felts over 300 psi (20.7 bar); 3-4 in from the felt; see [[hp-shower-nozzles-and-oscillation]] [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.19] [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.24]
- Cap batt: finer denier is smoother and holds the sheet, but plugs and wears faster; sheet follows the felt with finer cap [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.17] [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.19]
- Permeability declines through the felt cycle; chemical cleaning must be optimised for dense felts [DOC 39e95278-beb1-4937-867a-db98ca39f611 p.22]
- Hubbe 2020: felt voids fill with pitch and minerals; cleaning programs and model-based replacement timing; see [[wet-press-dewatering-practice]] [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.55]
