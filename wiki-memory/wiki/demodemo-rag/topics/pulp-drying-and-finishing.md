- "Market pulp is sold by the air dry ton, which is made up of 90% bone dry fiber and 10% water"; deliveries 90-95% air dry (81-86% oven dry) [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.21]
- Wetlap: roll presses raise consistency to 40-45%; Fig 9-60 double-wire press up to 40%, heavy press to 48-50% [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.21] [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.22]
- Drying makes fibre walls rigid and less swellable, lowering burst and tensile; not entirely reversible by refining [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.21]
- Air float dryer: air impingement above and below web, web floats at about H ~ 2 mm (Fig 9-64), economizer recovers exhaust heat [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.23]
- Area evaporation rate (lb/sq ft/hr): 0.9-1.0 older mechanically conveyed, 1.7-1.8 air impingement; steam economy 1.2-1.7 lb steam/lb water [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.24]
- Higher evaporation rate favoured by light sheet, high in/out moisture, high air temperature, high makeup air, low stock pH (Table 9-6) [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.24]
- Threading: older dryers use a 6-10 inch tail and "kite"; newer use twin bands or folded tape; turning rolls driven only during threading [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.23]
- Flash drying used mainly for mechanical market pulp; lower capital, higher operating cost; early problems brightness loss and pills, fluffer critical [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.25]
- Cutter and layboy: slitters, fly knife, layboy stacks, bales wrapped and wired [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.24]
