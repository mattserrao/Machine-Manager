- Brightness measured on a Zeiss Elrepho diffuse reflectance meter; fully bleached sulfite about 94, unbleached kraft as low as about 15 [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.28]
- Table 11-1 unbleached brightness: kraft 15-30; NSSC and ammonium bisulfite 40-50; groundwood, bisulfite, sulfite 50-65 [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.28]
- Colour comes mainly from lignin chromophores; Fe and Cu form coloured complexes with phenolic groups [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.28]
- Monitoring: kappa number (lignin), viscosity (cellulose chain length), brightness [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.28]
- Codes: C chlorination, H hypochlorite, E extraction, D0/D1/D2 ClO2; O oxygen, EO/EOP enriched extraction, P peroxide, Pa peracetic acid, Q chelation, N neutralization, X enzymes, Y hydrosulfite, Z ozone [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.29]
- 1970s kraft standard CEDED; 1980s (C+D)(EO)DED; North America today D(EO)DED or D(EOP)DED [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.29]
- Chlorine/hypochlorite use fell sharply in the 1990s due to AOX and chloroform; hypochlorite still used in dissolving pulp for CED viscosity [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.29]
- ECF made 67% of world bleached chemical pulp in 2000 (80% in North America); TCF only 7% [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.30]
- Sulfite and hardwood kraft bleach more easily than softwood kraft [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.30]
- Lignin-preserving brightening gains are limited to the 70s (above 80 possible) and are not permanent: [[mechanical-pulp-brightening]] [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.28]
