- Kappa number monitors cooking and bleaching; other pulp tests: CED viscosity, alpha cellulose, fibre length [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.20]
- Fibre length classified into five fractions on progressively finer screens; Kajaani analyzer counts fibres in 144 length categories using a 0.001% consistency suspension through a glass capillary [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.21]
- Fines (passing 200 mesh) are the main cause of reduced drainage [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.21]
- Britt dynamic drainage jar: propeller motor 0 to 3000 rpm; used for drainage on the forming wire, first-pass retention and retention aids [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.22]
- Standard freeness does not reproduce shear on the wire: groundwood gives lower freeness than highly beaten chemical pulp but drains faster on the machine [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.22]
- Laboratory beating is gentler than mill refining; idealized beating (research, market pulp) vs mill simulation beating (process control) [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.22]
- Valley beater: hard to reproduce between units over time due to uneven bar wear [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.23]
- PFI mill: grooved roll in smooth trough, 10% consistency, little metal contact so rarely needs calibration [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.23]
- Lampen mill: 250 rpm, 10-kg ball, consistency 3.0% softwood and 3.6% hardwood [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.23]
- Handsheets per TAPPI T205; strength compared at reference freeness 500 CSF and 300 CSF by interpolating the beater curve [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.24]
- Tear-tensile plots are the main comparison tool; need a 3- or 4-point beater run, single-point test may suffice for similar pulps [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.24]
## AFT manual pulp tests ([[doc-aft-intro-stock-prep-refining]])
- Moisture: dry sheets 5-15%, wet lap 50-60%; consistency in stock prep refining 2-6%. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.14]
- Fines: particles under 0.2 mm (or P200 on Bauer McNett); kraft 5-15%, groundwood may exceed 40%. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.15]
- Handsheets are fully restrained during drying (no X-Y shrinkage), unlike machine paper; Valley Beater samples every 5 or 10 minutes over 20-40 minutes. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.16]
- Kappa, viscosity, fibre length (Kajaani FS-200, Optest FQA), coarseness in mg/m, zero-span: [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.14] [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.15]
