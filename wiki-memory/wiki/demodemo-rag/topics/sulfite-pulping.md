## Chemistry and terms
- Tilghman discovered sulfurous acid pulping in 1857 (US patent 1867); first commercial sulfite pulp Sweden 1874; kraft overtook it in the 1930s [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.20]
- North American sulfite mills fell from 64 (1980) to 40 (1990) to 23 (2000); by base in 2000: Ca 2, Mg 7, Na 9, NH3 5 [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.20]
- One calcium bisulfite equals one free SO2 plus one combined SO2: "Ca(HSO3)2 -> CaSO3 + H2SO3"; "true free SO2" = free minus combined = total minus 2 x combined [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.20]
- Table 6-2 samples A/B/C all total SO2 10 g/100 mL; true free SO2 0, 4, 0 respectively [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.21]
- pH: acid bisulfite 1.5-4.0; true bisulfite about 4.0-5.0; calcium acid cooks about 1.5 (higher pH scales and "limes" the digester) [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.21]
- Liquor made by burning sulfur to SO2 and absorbing in an alkaline solution (limestone, NH4OH, Mg(OH)2 or Na2CO3) [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.21]

## Cook
- Raw acid total SO2 4.0-4.2; cooking acid total 6.0-8.0, free 5.0-6.8, combined 1.0-1.2 (g/100 mL presumed) [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.22]
- "Slow come-up to 130-140 C, with an overall cook of 6-8 hours, is typical for acid sulfite" (notes); reaction starts about 110 C [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.22]
- Blow when 1-1.5 h of cook left; contents blown at about 20-25 psig; stop point judged by kappa/permanganate number, liquor colour and residual SO2 [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.24]
- Lignin polycondensation ("burnt" cook) is caused by high acid and/or temperature; monovalent bases (Na, NH4) rarely cause it [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.25]
- SO2 made at above about 1100 C with about 10% excess oxygen to avoid SO3 [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.25]

## Bases, two-stage, outlook
- Cook rate order at same pH/temp/SO2: ammonium fastest, then magnesium, sodium, calcium; strength is essentially independent of base at identical acid composition [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.26]
- Table 6-5 two-stage pH: two-stage acid (Mg) 5.5 then 1.5-2.0; magnefite (Mg) 3-4 then 6.0-6.5; Stora (Na) 6 then 3-4; Sivola (Na) 3-4 then 9-10 [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.26]
- World share of traditional sulfite fell from about 20% (1960) to under 3% (2000); interest moved to alkaline sulfite-AQ and ASAM [DOC 12a3bba4-9144-478f-9140-9e24c40c2051 p.27]
