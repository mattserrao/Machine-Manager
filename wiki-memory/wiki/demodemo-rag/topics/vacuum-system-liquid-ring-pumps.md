- Vacuum system needs capacity for all vacuum-assisted elements: forming and press suction boxes and felt-conditioning boxes [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.4]
- Sources: centrifugal exhausters and rotary lobe pumps; "Most common: positive-displacement liquid-ring pump" giving full-range vacuum and handling mixed water and air [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.4]
- Fig 16-92 system has eight pumps; a flanged vacuum header isolates different vacuum levels and allows shared service if one pump is down [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.4]
- Water-ring pump has three sectors: liquid moves outward drawing gas in; liquid moves inward compressing gas; compressed gas leaves through discharge ports [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.5]
- The rotor is the only moving part, body cast iron, the sealing liquid is the compressant (Nash Engineering) [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.5]
- Pumps sit in the basement along a vacuum header with a stack to atmosphere; discharge trench and sump collect water [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.5]

## Performance evaluation (TAPPI TIP 0404-55) - [[doc-tappi-tip-0404-55-liquid-ring-vacuum-evaluation]]
- Best mill test is the flat plate orifice test on an isolated pump; boroscope gives a rougher estimate. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.1]
- Iron rotor-cone pumps lose 30-50% capacity over 10-20 years; stainless 5-10% over ten years. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.2]
- Tune seal water to "maximum stable vacuum" (needle just stops fluttering) using a linear regulating valve. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.2-3]
- Wasted cost = annual cost x (1 - % original capacity); 86% pump example wastes 30.1 kW of 215 kW. [DOC a92ccac2-eca7-4b0a-a2cc-bd07d3da9fa5 p.3-4]
- Sweet best practices: seal water flow can vary plus or minus 10-20% with little impact; cooling-tower recirculated seal water is where pump rebuilds concentrate; see [[vacuum-pump-seal-water]] [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.7]
- Vacuum system power can often be cut 10-20% (one case 1.2 MW, 40%): [[excess-vacuum-capacity]]; uhle box airflow sizing 15-20 cfm per in2 slot: [[uhle-box-sizing]] [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5, p.8-9]
