## Formulas and values (quoted)
- "DR = (Cv - Cs) / (Cv - Cw)" (Cv vat liquor solids, Cw wash liquor solids, Cs sheet solids) [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.27]
- "TF = (W_in - W_out) / W_in", W = (100 - consistency)/consistency; "% efficiency = [TF + (1 - TF).DR] x 100" [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.27]
- DF = (mass shower water - mass water leaving with pulp) / mass OD pulp [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.25]
- Typical specific loading 0.7-0.8 BDTPD/ft2; higher loading in expanded mills worsens washing [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.25]
- Single displacement stage rarely above about 80%; 3-4 stages for about 99% of washable solids [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.25]
- 99% efficiency corresponds to about 15-20 lb washable saltcake carryover per BDT [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.27]
- Vacuum washer discharge consistency 12-14% [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.25]
- Norden factor examples (Table 9-2): vacuum drum washer 2.5-4, single-stage diffuser 3-5 [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.3]

## Practice
- Countercurrent staging: cleanest water to last stage, filtrate of each stage washes the previous; Fig 9-6 three washers, foam tank [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.26]
- Key variables: specific loading, dilution factor, air in stock; higher DF raises evaporation load; large seal tanks and foam breakers manage foam [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.27]
- Diffusion washing: Kamyr atmospheric diffusers 1965, pressure diffusers 1979; high-heat wash zone up to 4 h at 130-140 C; atmospheric diffuser 8-10 min per stage; sawdust digester wash about 20 min [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.27] [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.28]
- Fig 9-9: DF 3.0 reaches about 99.5% at about 5 h; DF 1.5 only about 96-97% [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.29]
- Pressure washers (Beloit Rauma) allow 2-3 stages per washer, hotter wash liquor, less foam; Drum Displacer 2-4 stages [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.29]
- Other washers: compaction baffle, horizontal belt, extraction press, dilution/extraction [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.2]

## Defibering and deknotting
- Knots are pulp retained on a 3/8 in perforated plate; typically 2-4% of production; repulped knots give 10-30% lower yield; high knots signal poor cooking uniformity [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.23]
- Fig 9-2: modification of liquor circulation cut knotter rejects from about 2.4% to about 1.0% of pulp [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.24]
- Pressure knotters (enclosed, foil pulses) replacing vibrating knotters; rejects need secondary screening [DOC b439a355-b41d-42a4-9c04-727066254493 p.23]
