## Strategies
- Five strategies: use available sources incl. alternate fuels; raise thermodynamic efficiency; cut losses and waste; recover and reuse energy; change processes to use less energy [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.1]
- An energy audit (energy balance) is needed before improving an existing plant [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.1]
- Within a steam cycle: limit boiler losses, keep back pressure at the minimum the process needs, maximize condensate return; higher steam pressure and temperature improve plant efficiency [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.1]
- A mill-wide balance shows most energy leaving through few streams, notably paper machine dryer exhaust and warm effluents; excessive drying steam use is common [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.1]
- Two water-reduction methods: closing up the paper machine water system, and counter-current washing in the bleach plant [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.1]
- Recovery opportunities: lime kiln product coolers, paper machine economizers, blow heat recovery, bleach plant effluent heat exchangers [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.1]
- Woodroom example: belt conveyors replaced pneumatic chip transport; one source says a belt conveyor motor uses about one-eighth the energy of a blower [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.2]
