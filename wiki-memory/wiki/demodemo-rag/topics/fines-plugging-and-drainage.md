- Fines definition: "cellulosic fines are expected to be able to pass through a circular opening of 76 um" (TAPPI T 261 cm-94) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.16]
- Demonstration: heavily refined kraft has greatly lowered freeness; after Bauer-McNett removal of fines, freeness "typically will again resemble that of unrefined or slightly refined pulp" [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.34]
- Mechanism (Fig. 9): fines not firmly attached to fibers, small enough to be carried by flow yet large enough to lodge, especially in densified layers; flow needs at least one contiguous open channel (percolation theory) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.34-35]
- Fibrillar fines made by refining kraft hurt dewatering far more than parenchyma cells already in the pulp; finer fraction means worse effect [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.35]
- Fines sensitivity grows with basis weight (Brundrett and Baines, Gess, Wildfong, Paradis); Chang found hydraulic pressure in simulated pressing rose strongly with basis weight [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.35]
- Gravity drainage test: filtration resistance maximum at 0.5% consistency; fines above 15% slowed drainage (Mantar 1995) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.28]
- Hydrofoils wash fines from the wire side; retention aids make z-direction fines distribution more uniform [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.35]
- Remedy: retention aid program attaches each fine to a fiber (Fig. 10); Britt and Unbehend benefits only after floc-breaking agitation; nanofibrillated cellulose slowed drainage but was overcome by optimizing retention aids (Balea 2019) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.36]
- Alternative explanation: Kozeny-Carman resistance rises with the square of specific surface area; fines behave as 2 to 20 times the specific surface of kraft fibers (Marton); Szikla (1986) found plugging not important in the wet-press systems studied [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.36]
- Fines retained per pass: "a substantial proportion of the fines will be retained in the paper during each pass" [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.17]
