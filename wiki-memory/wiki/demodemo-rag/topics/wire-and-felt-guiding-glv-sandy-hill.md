## How it works
- Palm rides on the fabric edge; palm motion moves a needle valve that sends air to one of two diaphragms on the actuator, moving the guide roll end to recentre the fabric. [DOC 5e262f9c-c4c3-43d7-9951-7107e3ff6b83 p.1]
- Fabric runs off in CD, valve moves smoothly (not in steps) to one of two pressure/exhaust positions, then back to balance when centred. [DOC 5e262f9c-c4c3-43d7-9951-7107e3ff6b83 p.2]

## Start-up and after fabric changes
- Lock actuator at centre with the locking pin, set air by mounting (horizontal 30-75 psi; vertical 60-75; at angle 30-75), centre via hand guide, pull pin, test direction by pushing the palm away. [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]
- Start slowly and check guiding as speed rises, at initial start-up and after fabric changes. [DOC 41ed1702-c12c-4bb2-bc78-880c068da1a4 p.1]

## Preventative maintenance (machine down, lockout/tagging)
- Minimum yearly: rebuild and recalibrate controller; inspect needle bearings (max life 3 years, inspect at least yearly); ways; air diaphragms; palm; air connections and lines; hand guide slide blocks. [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.1] [DOC e4520346-07b0-490e-bbd1-b8e8d20af3a5 p.2]
