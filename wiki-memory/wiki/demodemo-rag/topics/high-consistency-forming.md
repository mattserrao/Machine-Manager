- Compressive strength rises quickly with z-direction bonding up to a level, then slowly [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.37]
- High-consistency formed (3-6%) pilot medium: about +20% in CMT and compressive strength, large Scott bond gain; burst and tensile about 33% lower [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.37]
- Low MD tensile could hurt fluting and cause scoreability problems; may promote flute fracture unless compensated e.g. by a low-friction surface [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.37-38]
- Dewaters readily (small water load, open structure) and was about 2% drier after the press section at same density [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.38]
- Table IV conventional / high consistency / difference: basis weight 152/151 g/m2 (-0.7%); density 550/560 kg/m3 (+1.8%); tensile index MD 60/33 (-45.0%), CD 30/26 (-13.3%), geometric 43/29 (-32.6%); burst index 1.8/1.2 (-33.3%); tear index 6.0/6.5 (+8.3%); CMT30 230/280 N (+21.7%); bending stiffness 3.0/3.0 (0.0%); compressive strength 15/18 MPa (+20.0%); Scott bond 200/420 J/m2 (+110.0%) [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.38]
