## Procedure (13 steps, p.2)
- Safety first; discuss with the crew that a press solids sample is to be taken during the next wet end break. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.2]
- Label a one-gallon Ziplock bag; avoid ingoing nips and wads going into press nips, dryer nips, blow box nips, doctor blade holders. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.2]
- Hold normal conditions: former and press at normal operating conditions, stock blend normal if possible, presses at normal setpoints, steamboxes/showers/vacuum at normal setpoints. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.2]
- Cut a narrow tail (3-4 inches wide) if possible; use a poly sample wand (with or without cup); do not break the plane of the press with arms, hands, body. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.2]
- Take a tennis-ball-sized sample, bag it immediately; document grade, speed, press loading, press felt age; send to lab; thread up as normal. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.2]

## Calculation (p.3)
- Wet and dry sample weights are net of bag weight; % solids = dry sample weight / wet sample weight x 100. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.3]
- "Sample should stay in oven for 4 hours or until dry"; break the sample up first for even drying. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.3]

## Tips (p.4)
- Split the sample into 2-3 separate samples on tared aluminum plates, dried filter paper, or torn copier paper; a tight clump does not dry well. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.4]
- Averaged samples have more power than a single sample; more consistent samples beat very few elaborate ones. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.4]
- Part of the sample away from the edge is likely closer to actual sheet moisture. [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.4]
- Dry weight = two consecutive weights with no more decrease; some machines use heated plates or the tailcutter sample; sampling off the 1st dryer is also done (slightly affected, often safer). [DOC bb3f776f-3519-4875-88bc-e6524716d692 p.4]
