- Screen types: vibratory, gravity centrifugal, pressure (centrifugal, centripetal or combined flow); rotating foil gives negative pulse that clears openings [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.3] [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.5]
- Holes now usually taper-drilled; slots cut or wedge-wire; contoured cylinders add microturbulence and allow lower reject rates [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.6]
- Performance measures: debris reject efficiency E_R, cleanliness efficiency E_C, Nelson's Screening Quotient Q; efficiency depends strongly on reject rate and plate opening; Table 9-3 lists variables [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.7]
- Cascade: three-stage cascade with stock balance (Fig 9-28); two-stage primary system for TMP (Fig 9-29) [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.8]
- Cleaners run at reject rates of about 10-20%; plugging is the main operating problem; elutriation ("stock saver") recovers fibre [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.9] [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.10]
- Fig 9-37 typical consistencies: forward feed 0.6% accepts 0.5% rejects 2.0%; reverse-flow feed 1.0% rejects 0.4% accepts 1.7%; through-flow feed 1.0% accepts 1.0% rejects 0.2% [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.12]
- Forward cleaners remove heavy debris (SG above 1.0); reverse-flow and through-flow remove light debris in secondary fibre; through-flow displaced reverse-flow after mid-1980s (lower pressure drop, low reject rates) [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.12]
- Medium-consistency screening 8-15% avoids dilution below 10% in TMP/CTMP/CMP lines [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.18]
