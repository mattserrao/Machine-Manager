- Vacuum causes compression dewatering, and after the dry line displacement dewatering (air forcing water out) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.10]
- Lo-vac boxes create vacuum from water flowing down tubes to a seal chest; a vacuum pump marks the demarcation to high-vacuum boxes; vacuum conventionally increased in successive boxes [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.10]
- Slots 13 to 25 mm wide; vacuum pulse half a ms to several ms; twin-wire equivalents are "suction shoes" [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.11]
- Constant vacuum gives moisture tending to an asymptote; dryness vs dwell time is a hyperbolic tangent (Jones 1998) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.11]
- Koponen: LWC solids 11% at 25 kPa; SC fibers 14% at 40 kPa; repeated compression-expansion between boxes is beneficial [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.11]
- Leading-edge doctoring skims water from underside of the fabric ahead of the dry line [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.11]
- Problems: drive friction between fabric and covers, back-side fabric abrasion (sand in cracks of covers), pinholes from entrained air (about 51 um or larger, Brundrett and Baines), wire marking [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.11]
- Non-uniform formation wastes vacuum: air leaks through thin areas; MFC addition cut air flow and raised solids after forming in pilot work [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.12]
- Vacuum couch roll: no flatbox drag, higher vacuum possible, solids "15 to 23%"; limits are couch marking and centrifugal acceleration; avoid marking by keeping enough solids after flatboxes [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.12]
- Capillary breakthrough: delta P = 2 gamma / R (Eq. 14); newsprint pulp (more fines, small R) has much higher breakthrough pressure than bleached sulfite [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.39-40]
- Surfactants cut surface tension: nonionic (Lindqvist) and SDS (Lehmonen 2020) raised dryness; warmer water also helps; not widely practiced because of foam and sizing concerns [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.41]
- Membrane tests: air breakthrough is not essential up to a point (Brundrett, Aslund and Vomhoff); Raisanen found air passage unfavorable [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.41]
- "there is no benefit to be expected from applying very high vacuum, above a certain level, e.g. 40 kPa" (Attwood 1960) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.42]
- Through-air-dried tissue uses displacement dewatering with hot air instead of pressing [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.42]
- Electricity: vacuum boxes may use one-fifth of machine electricity [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.2]
