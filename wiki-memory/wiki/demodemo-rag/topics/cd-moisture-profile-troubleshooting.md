## Start
- First step: FLIR the sheet for uneven temperature to identify where on the machine the variation occurs. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]

## Causes listed, in checklist order
- Scanner: dirty, mapping, edge sensing. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]
- Steambox: mapping, actuator air lines mismatched, failed actuators, condensation. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]
- Rewet shower: mapping, leaks, plugged nozzles. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]
- Dryer can buildup: review doctor cans for streaks; brass or abrasive doctor blades for cleaning need a "Safety review needed before. This can cause fires". [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]
- Dryer felt permeability: shine a flashlight into the felt run and look down from the top for plugged areas; a clean-looking surface does not mean it is not plugged internally. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]
- Dryer felt may be pressure washed: do NOT use a 0 degree nozzle, do not hold the wand in one place, do not hit the fabric with the nozzle. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]
- Wet felt permeability: showering, burning (skinning doctor, Uhle box lube, roll cover failure/uneven wear, wads through nips), batt loss. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]
- Sleeve failure: FLIR shows a heated area if delamination or damage has occurred. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]
- Wire permeability: may not be visible in the wet line but seals the sheet there; plugged showers; remedy with pressure washer. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.1]

## Wire pressure washing
- Much more effective with hot water. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.2]
- Do NOT use a 0 degree nozzle (damages wire); use a fanned nozzle. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.2]
- Wand jumps when the trigger is pulled or released; do not let it hit the wire. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.2]

## Headbox
- Lexan sheet failure and plugged header holes both "appear like a snake on table". [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.2]
- No impingement or uneven impingement at the forming board. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.2]
- Warped slice lip: causes over-cranking of slice adjustment screws, temperature; check with a Teflon feeler gauge, never metal. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.2]
- Warped apron: breast roll pluggage or a wire wreck can shove debris between breast roll and apron; check with laser or feeler gauge between bottom of apron and breast roll. [DOC dcf8af26-db8b-4545-b1a6-80cb9239b6c5 p.2]
