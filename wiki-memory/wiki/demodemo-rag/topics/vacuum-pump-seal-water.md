Source document: [[doc-vacuum-dewatering-best-practices-part-1]]. Related: [[vacuum-system-liquid-ring-pumps]], [[excess-vacuum-capacity]].

- Seal water is "generally ignored until significant problems are causing vacuum issues" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.6]
- Problems: no understanding of operation, little/no instrumentation of flow or pressure, little/no monitoring of recirculated water quality, poor cooling tower condition [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.6]
- Liquid ring pumps run at relatively low speed and seal water must flow continuously; each model has a standard flow; "Too little water decreases pump performance; too much water adds to pump horsepower" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.7]
- "The standard rate can vary by plus or minus 10-20 percent with little impact on performance. Therefore, precise flow control is unnecessary." [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.7]
- Flow is generally set with spray nozzles and orifices chosen for a supply pressure; verify sizes with the pump manufacturer; local pressure gauges needed; color-coded gauges help (Fig. 5-6) [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.7]
- Clean once-through seal water: little or no pump maintenance; recirculation through cooling towers is "usually in the worst shape, requiring frequent pump rebuilds" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.7]
- Cooling tower problems: incorrect fill, no working separators, undersized separators, separator extraction pump issues, overloaded water filters [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.7-8]
- Towers are outside the machine room, often with no process owner, and problems "are usually not noticed until they become serious" [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.8]
- Best practices: know flow requirements; orifices/nozzles with pressure measurement or flow measurement per pump; working local gauges; working separators and extraction pumps; "Daily or at least weekly inspection of cooling tower"; simple analysis (pH, conductivity, turbidity) watching for change [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.8]
