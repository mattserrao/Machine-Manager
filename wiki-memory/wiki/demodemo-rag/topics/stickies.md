- A sticky particle is typically hydrophobic, tacky and depositable, and often deposits on equipment. [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.2]
- Stickies adsorb particulates/dissolved species (talc, calcium carbonate, inks, toners, polymers). [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.2]
- Believed to be a mixture of hot melt adhesives, PSAs, glues, wax, inks, paper additives, coatings, wood extractives. [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.3]
- "The actual surface behavior of stickies depends on the adsorbed species." [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.4]
- Hot melts: vinyl acetate polymers and copolymers, tackifiers, wax. [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.5]
- Control and removal: [[stickies-control-and-removal]]; measurement: [[stickies-detection-methods]]. Summary: deck covers deposits, holes and web breaks, screening, cleaners, DAF, additives, dispersion, macro/micro test methods. [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.1]

## Properties and classes (doc pages 6-14, filed from batch library-2)
- Hot melts: 100 % solid, "Soften at 150°F to 250°F", insoluble in aqueous media, soluble in many organic solvents, density 0.9 to 1.0 g/cc [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.6]
- PSA composition: rubber elastomer (styrene-butadiene or poly-acrylate), tackifiers, fillers (MgO, ZnO) [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.7]
- PSA: tacky at room temperature, density 0.9 to 1.1 g/cc, often precipitate with drops in pH [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.8]
- Primary stickies are formed in the pulper; secondary stickies form from a change in environment, pH, temperature [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.9]
- Macrostickies are retained on a 6-cut slotted screen; "6 cut = 0.006 inches (0.15 mm) (150 micrometers)"; trend towards 0.10 mm or 0.075 mm slots; microstickies pass it [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.10]
- Size scale (log, 0.01 to 1000 um): dissolved, colloidal, dispersed, suspended, macro; microstickies span dissolved through suspended [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.11]
- Macro stickies are large, can be screened, visible and offensive in products [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.12]
- Micro stickies are small, carried along with water, cannot be screened, very chemistry sensitive (agglomerate, precipitate), not generally seen in product; water clarification can be effective [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.12]

## Problems
- Product quality: dirty spots, holes, picks; converting issues in coating, printing, folding, scoring, cutting [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.13]
- Operations: deposition on machine, web breaks, downtime for cleaning [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.13]
- Stickies adhere to forming fabrics and press felts and build up at doctor blades and rolls, causing holes and breaks; can reduce clothing life [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.51]
- "Sticky removal is one of the most critical parts of recovered paper processing." [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.51]
- Board machines: stickies contaminate clothing, cause holes/tears and contaminate printing blankets [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.13]
- Government push for environmentally benign adhesive (EBA): EO 13148 signed April 21, 2000 mandates EBA for government purchases; all USPS stamps are EBA; cost circa $100,000,000 in United States [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.17]
