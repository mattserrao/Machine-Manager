Sources: [[doc-smook-fundamentals-part-06]] p.30 and [[doc-smook-fundamentals-part-07]] pp.1-3. ClO2 specifics: [[chlorine-dioxide-generation]].

- All conventional bleaching chemicals come from air, water and brine; energy is the main cost driver [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.30]
- Oxygen: cryogenic separation for suppliers and mills above about 80 t/day; pressure-swing adsorption (molecular sieves) for small on-site plants [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.30]
- Cell types: mercury, asbestos diaphragm (dilute caustic with NaCl), membrane (concentrated caustic) [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.30]
- Membrane cell is the most recent and used in most new plants; essentially pure NaOH [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.1]
- Caustic shipped as 50% solution, stored at 50% and diluted to 5% (control by density or conductivity); chlorine shipped as liquid [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.1]
- Decline of chlorine bleaching unbalanced chlor-alkali demand and encouraged non-electrolytic caustic by causticizing sodium carbonate [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.1]
- Hypochlorite made at mill: "2NaOH + Cl2 -> NaOCl + NaCl + H2O"; excess alkali required to avoid decomposition to chlorate [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.1]
- Chlorate: brine electrolysis, net "NaCl + 3H2O + Electrical Energy -> NaClO3 + 3H2" [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.2]
- Peroxide mostly by anthraquinone autoxidation; H-D Tech electrolytic route gives 30-80 g/L [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.3]
- Ozone by corona discharge from oxygen with carrier recovery; peracids (peracetic, Caro's acid) cannot be transported and are made on site [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.3]
