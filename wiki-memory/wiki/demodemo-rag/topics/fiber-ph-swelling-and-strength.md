## Mechanism
- Fibre is a cellulosic gel with bound acid groups; counter-ions stay inside, osmotic pressure draws water in and the fibre swells (Donnan theory) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.17]
- Carboxyl groups pKa 3-5; xylan side group (4-O-methyl-glucuronic acid) pKa about 3 [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.12, p.15]
- Swelling rises with pH to a plateau at pH 4-5 to 10, then falls as ionic strength rises [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.19]
- Swelling depends on: amount of acid groups, their dissociation constant, counter-ion type, pH, ionic concentration, fibre wall elastic modulus/flexibility, degree of beating [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.20]
- Counter-ion order for swelling: "H+ < Ca2+ < Na+"; sodium-form pulp beats faster and gives higher tensile at a given beating level [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.18]
- Beating raises accessible surface charge but not total charge [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.15]
- Hammar et al.: best beatability at pH 9; low ionic strength during beating gives better beatability and tensile [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.19]

## Findings by pulp (thesis)
- NSSC (highest charge, 20-25% xylan): pH above 3 raised charge, WRV, tensile, stiffness, SCT, CCT at 2000 rev; pH 3 significantly lowest [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.44]
- Bleached softwood kraft: smaller effect, seen only at 4000 rev; BSW needs more beating (higher specific edge load) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.37]. See [[specific-edge-load]].
- Bleached hardwood kraft: no effect, attributed to higher fibre stiffness resisting swelling [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.7]
- Unbeaten pulps: no effect; pH and beating are additive [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.40]
- Mill trial NSSC pH 4.8 vs 5.6: no significant change in mechanical properties; both above pKa so charge is already about 86-98% dissociated [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.41]
- Practical caution from trial: lowering pH with sulfuric acid risks H2S and SO2 gas (trial stopped at pH 4.8) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.30]
- Suggested follow-up: test raising NSSC pH above 5 at mill scale, effect on dewatering and runability [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.45]

## Charge measurement
- Surface charge by titrating with cationic poly-DADMAC (0.001 N) to zero streaming potential in a Mutek PCD: q = c*V/wt (Eq.1) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.16, p.26]
- Surface:bulk charge ratio about 0.05 [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.17]
- Single measurements, results "somewhat uncertain" (BHW shows more charge than BSW, may be artefact) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.38]

## Bonding discussion (DOC fc581f72, discussion p.35-37)
- Stenius: differences in fibre-fibre bonding between similar pulps with different pulping/bleaching are "mainly due to flexibility and the relative bond area" [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.35]
- Wagberg and Stenius agree light scattering is not a measure of bond strength; no good method yet [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.35]
- Swelling vs charge: at 0.5 mol/l NaCl still correlated with charge; extrapolated to zero charge there is residual swelling "probably due to hemicelluloses" [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.36]
- Annergren: to make strong paper from a 90 kappa pulp "you have to beat like hell" [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.37]
- Charge also affects hornification (extent decreases as ionizable groups increase) [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.29]
