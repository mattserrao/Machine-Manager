## Converting scope
- About 75% of North American paper and paperboard output undergoes some converting before reaching the final customer [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.3]
- Corrugated box plants use the most paper and paperboard tonnage; other box and container plants are second [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.3]
- Wet converting (roll form): coating, laminating, corrugating, impregnating, embossing, wet creping. Dry converting: bag, box and envelope making, cutting and folding, hot pressing, die cutting, roll winding (Table 23-2) [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.3]

## Functional coatings
- Functional coatings are non-aqueous and add barriers against water, water vapour, air and grease; materials are waxes, asphalt, lacquer, varnish, resins, adhesives [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.3]
- Extrusion coater (Fig. 23-7): molten resin, usually polyethylene, pushed through a slot die as a hot viscous film and bonded to the paper between a pair of rolls; no counterpart in the paper mill [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.3]
- Hot melts (polyolefin and wax mixtures) are too viscous for ordinary coaters but too thin for extrusion coaters, so the curtain coater (Fig. 23-8) is used: coating falls as a vertical curtain through which the sheet runs at high speed [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.4]
- Gravure-type coater: for light applications of solvent-based coatings; etched gravure roll, doctor blade removes excess [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.4]
- Laminating joins two or more webs; asphalt is common because it is both adhesive and barrier [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.4]
- Impregnating is used for decorative laminates, gasket papers, oiled papers and roofing felts [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.5]

## Corrugated board
- Single-face = fluted medium on one liner; double-face = fluted medium between two liners (most common); double-wall and triple-wall are stronger and stiffer [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.4]
- Four standard flutes A, B, C and E; newer small/mini/fine profiles are labelled E, F, G and N [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.4] [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.5]
- Process: medium preheated and moistened, flute former, adhesive on flute tips bonds to a liner (single-face), bridge accumulates web for reel changes, second glue layer joins the double-face liner, bond set in heating section, cooled and cut to blanks [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.5]
- Corrugator speeds: earlier up to 150 m/min, newer 300 m/min, next generation designed for 400 m/min [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.5]
- Starch adhesives remain in use; trend to lightweight grades with lower-grammage liners and medium [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.5]
- Folded sanitary products: method 1 cuts one or a few webs with a rotary knife then folds; method 2 folds many webs first, gathers a wad, then cuts to package length [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.6]
