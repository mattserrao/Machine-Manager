## Causticizing
- Sequence: smelt dissolved in weak wash -> green liquor -> clarified (dregs removed) -> lime (CaO) forms white liquor -> clarified (lime mud CaCO3 removed) [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.14]
- Reactions: CaO + H2O -> Ca(OH)2 + heat (slaking); Ca(OH)2 + Na2CO3 -> 2 NaOH + CaCO3 (causticizing) [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.15]
- Typical mill causticizing efficiency runs 6-10% below equilibrium; equilibrium falls as total titratable alkali and sulphidity rise (Fig 10-28) [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.15]
- Three common operating problems: poor causticizing efficiency, insufficient dregs removal, poor settling or filtering of lime mud [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.17]
- Weak wash, not water, limits dilution when washing dregs [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.16]
- Table 10-3 lists variables (green liquor, lime, operating factors); Fig 10-34 lime mud precoat drum filter [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.18]

## Lime kiln
- Calcining CaCO3 -> CaO + CO2, three phases (drying, heating to about 800 C, holding); Table 10-4 total 7,600 thousand BTU per ton product [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.18]
- Lime mud soda kept below 1% Na2O; hot end about 1150-1250 C; integral tube coolers recover heat from discharged lime [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.20]
- Size up to 15 ft diameter, 450 ft long, L/D at least 30; slope 1/4 to 1/2 inch/ft (about 1.5 to 3.5 deg); 0.5-2.0 rpm; retention 2-4 h [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.21]
- Kiln gas dust: venturi scrubber most common, electrostatic precipitators preferred for largest kilns; fabric filters demonstrated but not adopted [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.21]
- Old short kilns can be "lengthened" by drying mud outside with flash dryer and cage mill (Fig 10-40) [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.21]
- Fluid bed calciner: compact, uniform pellets, fewer than 20 installations; flash calcining promoted by Fuller (over 25 systems) [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.21]
- Oxygen delignification with oxidized white liquor raises causticizing and kiln load 3-5%: [[oxygen-delignification]]
