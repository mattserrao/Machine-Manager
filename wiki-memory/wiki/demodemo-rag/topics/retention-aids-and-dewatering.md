- Crowding number N: related to square of fiber aspect ratio and linear in consistency; N = 1 frequent contact, 16 connected, 60 rigid network; headbox stock often 16 < N < 60 [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.31]
- Flockier formation gives faster initial dewatering, but uniform formation, fines retention and rapid initial dewatering "cannot be achieved simultaneously" (Kiviranta 1993) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.32]
- More uniform webs dewater better under vacuum [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.32]
- Britt and Unbehend: gravity drainage improved by rosin/alum, then PEI, then PEI plus very-high-mass anionic PAM; under vacuum the order reversed: untreated stock dewatered best, two-part flocculant sheets were wettest [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.32]
- Fix: apply sufficient hydrodynamic shear to the flocculated stock; best pilot-machine results for both early and vacuum dewatering [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.32]
- Practical option: add the high-mass retention aid before the pressure screen rather than after (Hubbe and Wang 2002) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.32]
- Cationic polymers promote early drainage; swelling and WRV drop with lower pH (about 6 to 3), higher ionic strength or multivalent cations [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.18-19]
- Davis et al. 1983 is cited as a rare clear case of chemical benefit under simulated wet-press conditions [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.36]
- Fillers adversely affected dewatering in a drainage/vacuum retention tester (Springer and Kuchibhotla 1992) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.28]
- Healing: at low consistency without flocculants, fibers fill thin areas within about 2 mm; Singh and Green found fines lowest at fabric knuckles, highest at fabric voids [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.5, p.31]
- Thickening vs filtration: structural pressure of fiber networks is below 1 kPa for consistencies under 15%; flow reversal (foils, flatbox leading edges) refluidizes the web but excess forming blade action cut strength [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.33]
