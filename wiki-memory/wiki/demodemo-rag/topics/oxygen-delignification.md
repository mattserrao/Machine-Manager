Source: [[doc-smook-fundamentals-part-07]] pp.7-8. Loads on [[recovery-boiler]] and [[causticizing-and-lime-kiln]].

- Continuation of pulping between digesters and the bleach plant; effluent compatible with kraft recovery; magnesium protector solved selectivity [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.7]
- A single stage can cut lignin by up to 50% [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.7]
- Recovery boiler solids load up about 4% (softwood) and 3% (hardwood); steam generation up only 1-2% [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.7]
- Oxidized white liquor for alkali raises causticizing and lime kiln load 3-5% [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.7]
- Started at high consistency (25-30%); medium consistency now dominant [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.7]
- Table 11-3: consistency 10-14%; 90-110 C; 80-125 psig; 20-60 min; pH 9.5-11; O2 1.5-2.2% on pulp; NaOH 1.5-3.5% on pulp; MgSO4 0.1 (as Mg) [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.8]
- Atmospheric-top variants suit sulfite pulps and give 30-35% lignin reduction on kraft [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.8]
- Oxygen-enriched extraction (EO): about 10 lb O2 per ton pulp, 5-10 min upflow tube though reaction usually done in 1-2 min [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.9]

## Surface effects
- Oxygen stage removes about 50% of total lignin but only about 15% of surface lignin and strongly raises water adhesion of the fibres; see [[fibre-charge-and-bonding]]. [DOC fc581f72-d049-4e5a-a59a-d3d6b1b6110c p.12, p.16]
