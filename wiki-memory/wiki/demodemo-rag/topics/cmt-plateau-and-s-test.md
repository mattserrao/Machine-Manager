## CMT procedure and problems
- Method: 10 MD strips 12.7 x 150 mm, lab fluter at 177 C (+-8 C), taped, crushed at once (CMT0) or after 30 min at 23 C / 50% RH (CMT30) [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.2]
- Maximum force before total collapse = CMTmax [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.2]
- Problems: slow, operator dependent, tape type and fluter condition matter, leaning flutes under-estimate strength or give no reading, worse at high basis weight/stiffness, delamination hard to interpret [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.2] [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.3]

## Why plateau
- The relevant point is when the board starts to lose elasticity, not total collapse; thickness is not sensitive to crush damage [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.1] [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.4]
- Flat crush hardness (first peak) fell 16% at 9% pre-crush and disappeared (100%) at 35% pre-crush while thickness changed 0% and 5% [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.4]
- Plateau is found where the first derivative of the force-compression curve crosses zero; if it does not cross, use displacement at the first minimum [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.6]
- CMT max can be equal for 175 g/m2 and 130 g/m2 fluting (questioning the dearer grade); plateau separates them [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.7]
- Starch at the size press raises both: 6-7 N per % on plateau vs 15 N per % on CMT max (recycled 90 g/m2); plateau response is "high enough to control a paper machine on strength" [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.8]

## S-test
- Modified L&W compressive strength tester (STFI): span 4 mm with 1 mm offset (S shape); automatable in L&W Autoline, no lab fluting [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.9]
- Correlation to CMT plateau high on 170 + 120 samples; clearly lower to CMT max [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.10]
- Smurfit Kappa Hoya online unit since May 2015: 97.6% correlation to real CMT plateau [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.11]
- Open: sensitivity to machine tuning, refining, starch levels, jet/wire ratio; verification on actual board and corrugator trials; one semi-chemical fluting deviated [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.9] [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.11]
- Standards cited: TAPPI T 809 (CMT), T 826 (SCT), T 825 (flat crush rigid support); ISO 7263, 9895, 3035 [DOC e3ade410-a412-462d-8429-86bc12165111 p.11]
