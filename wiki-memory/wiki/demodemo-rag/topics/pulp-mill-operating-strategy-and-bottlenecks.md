## Cost and scale
- Cost drivers: capital, chips, energy, chemicals, labor, transportation [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.16]
- Table 12-1 softwood bleached kraft 1994 US$/tonne totals: Chile 276, US Southeast 328, Canada West 361, New Zealand 422, Nordic 422, US West 444, Japan 598; fibre is the largest element [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.16]
- Table 12-2 hardwood totals: Indonesia 217, Brazil 231, Canada West 241, Chile 297, US Southeast 303, Nordic 337, Japan 488 [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.16]
- Minimum competitive size cited about 700 tons/day, later "well over 1000 tons/day" for new bleached kraft; CTMP and NSSC about 300 tons/day [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.17]
- Fig 12-3 relative capital cost per ton: about 200 at 100 t/d, about 100 near 500, about 60 near 900 [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.17]
- Rehabilitation or expansion is more common than greenfield; site schedule Fig 12-4 runs about 24 weeks to report [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.18]

## Buffers and upsets
- Production rate is usually coordinated to digester output; buffer storage levels kept at "about 40 to 60% of capacity" [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.18]
- Buffers hold roughly several hours; lasting problems force reduced rates and eventually shutdown (domino effect) [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.19]
- Liquor inventory control: track total active soda; raise makeup (saltcake, soda ash, caustic) if inventory falls [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.19]

## Lost time and bottlenecks
- Availability = fraction of time ready for full production; utilization = fraction actually at full production; utilization/availability near 1.0 marks a bottleneck, below about 0.8 spare capacity [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.19]
- Most established kraft mills are limited by recovery boiler capacity; it is the most expensive item and hard to expand [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.20]
- Easing the limit: lowest effective cooking chemical charge, high cooking liquor causticity; raising kappa target is "no longer acceptable" [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.20]
- Other examples: pulp drying (ship wetter pulp, more freight), digester impregnation time vs screen rejects [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.20]
