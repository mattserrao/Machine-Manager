Source document: [[doc-optimization-compressive-strength]] (pp.41-46). Related: [[corrugated-strength-tests]], [[converting-and-corrugated-board]], [[corrugating-medium-density-and-concora]], [[compressive-strength-units-and-test-conversions]].

- Survey finding: "increased density will increase the compressive strength and many other properties of linerboard and medium"; concern was that light-weight components fail by buckling so densification might not raise ECT [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.41]
- Analysis conclusion: "densification is beneficial for the lightweight structures as well" [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.41]
- Eq. 1: Pmy = k1(Pl1 + Pl2 + D Pc) + k2, where Pmy = combined board edgewise compressive strength, Pl1/Pl2 liners, Pc medium, D draw factor, k1/k2 empirical; accuracy about 7% [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.41]
- Plate-strength form (Eq. 2-3): liner and medium contributions use edgewise compressive strength and flexural stiffnesses Dx, Dy (MD, CD) with plate width W; average predictive accuracy "about 5%" over a broad range of constructions [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.42-44]
- "The exponent b was found to be equal to 0.75. This value is the same as used in the Institute box equation."; multiplying constants reflect converting technology of the time, new estimates "desirable" [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.44]
- Eq. 4-5: flexural stiffness D proportional to B^3/rho^r and compressive strength Pr proportional to rho^n B (B basis weight, rho density); r and n usually 1.5 to 2.5, often near 2 [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.44-45]
- Eq. 6-7: at constant basis weight Pl = K rho^(2b-1) W^(2b-1), i.e. Pl = K rho^0.5 W^0.5 for b = 0.75 [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.45]
- Result: raising density raises combined board ECT even with buckling, but by less than when buckling is ignored (Fig. 20) [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.45-46]
- Related Koning and Johnson et al. treat inelastic buckling and need empirical CD stress-strain curves [DOC 168553e0-baa3-4e06-827d-54415678796a p.42]
