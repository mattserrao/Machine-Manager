- Table 9-5 discharge consistency: slusher 3.5-4%; gravity thickener 4-8%; valveless filter 9-12%; vacuum filter 12-15% (great washing); multidisc filter 10-12%; screw extractor above 20%; presses above 20% [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.13]
- Below 0.7% to 10-12% may need two stages (slusher + valveless filter, Fig 9-42) [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.13]
- Multidisc thickeners are mostly used as saveall devices to recover fine fibre from white water; clear filtrate usable as shower water [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.15]
- Centrifugal pumps used "for stocks up to 6 - 7% consistency" [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.15]
- Medium-consistency pumping (Kamyr, early 1980s, Gullichsen) fluidizes suspensions up to 12% so they act like Newtonian fluids [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.17]
- High-density storage chest 12-15%, plug flow, zone agitation at bottom lowers consistency to uniform level below 4% [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.19]
- Blending chest after screening before bleaching ideal at 3-4 h retention; integrated mills store pulp at 10-14% [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.20]
