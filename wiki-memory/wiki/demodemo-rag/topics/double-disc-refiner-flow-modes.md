- Refiners are "hydraulic machines" acting like centrifugal pumps with very leaky wear plates; capacity is limited by net power and by volumetric flow. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.30]
- Double disc: floating rotor between two stators; position #1 is the stationary disc closest to the drive motor; three modes duo-flo, twin-flo, mono-flo. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.34]
- Equal refining on both sides needs equal closing force and equal flow; rotor must slide freely; sliding coupling, sliding bearing support and shaft packing box are "critical maintenance items". [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.35]
- Real disc pair pressure rise at zero flow is usually 1-2 bar (smooth stator would give 6-7 bar); peripheral speed about 90 ft/s; flow capacity varies about with diameter squared (42 in about 4.5 times 20 in). [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.35]
- Bar angle (pumping vs hold back) changes pumping efficiency but "No amount of hold back angle can produce a pressure drop at zero flow rate." [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.35]
- Duo-flo: two disc pairs are parallel pumps; imbalance is usually small and self-correcting; wear differences are almost always mechanical resistance to sliding. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.36]
- A 0.1 bar difference across the rotor faces can move about 40 kW: with 200 kW combined net, 80 kW on #1/#2 and 120 kW on #3/#4, intensity 50% higher on the #3/#4 pair. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.37]
- Keep pressure rise to about 1.5 bar or less; at low flow with high speed use a recycle flow; 2.0 bar rise acceptable if run at 80% or more of full power. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.37]
- Mono-flo (series): little or no net pressure rise (example 2.5 bar inlet, 4 bar casing, 2.3 bar outlet); about 0.2-0.3 bar pressure drop needed for uniform wear; usually needs recycle. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.37]
- Optimum intersecting bar angle "fixed at around 45o"; best control of mono-flo hydraulics is recycle flow, next bar height and groove/bar width ratio of #2 and #3 plates. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.39]
