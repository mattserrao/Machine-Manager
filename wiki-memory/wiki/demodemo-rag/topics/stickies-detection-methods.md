## Why it is hard
- "Sticky detection is incredibly difficult": must detect the particles and confirm they are tacky; low and intermittent concentrations; stickies can be passivated and undetectable; statistical significance often impossible; tests tedious [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.47]
- Basis of detection: stickies are typically synthetic hydrophobic polymers vs hydrophilic lignocellulosic fibre; tests use dye difference, deposition, melt vs decompose, tack, organic solvent solubility [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.48]
- Three categories: macro (large) methods, micro (small) methods, dissolved and colloidal methods [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.51]

## Macro stickies methods
- Bleaching and dyeing: bleach with chlorite, make handsheets, image analysis; dye with Morplas Blue, image analysis [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.53]
- Deposition: low consistency pulp slurry exposed to counter rotating paper machine wires; weigh deposits or dye and image analyse (Stickies Deposition Tester: motor, gears, paddle, water bath) [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.54]
- Tappi Test Method T-277: screen pulp with Pulmac Masterscreen (0.006 inch slots), collect rejects on black filter paper, press against white coated paper so adhesives pick up coating, darken fibre with black marker, image analysis [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.56]
- Port Townsend Method 1 (handsheets): make handsheets, dye with black ink, stickies do not pick up ink, image analysis [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.57]
- Port Townsend Method 2 (screening): Pulmac Masterscreen 0.006 inch slots, dye rejects with black ink, image analysis [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.58]
- Manual method: screen 100 g stock through 6-cut (0.150 mm), dry and press rejects against clean sheet, probe spots under microscope with pin, count sticky spots/100 g pulp; "tedious but can be very dependable compared to other methods" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.62]
- Other quantification names listed: fluorescent speck counting, Sulzer Escher Wyss, Southeast Paper, dye method [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.61]

## Effort and agreement (macro tests)
- Effort: bleaching/dyeing 4 hrs; deposition gravimetric 2 hrs, image analysis 3 hrs; Tappi T277 pm-99 1.5 hrs; Port Townsend 1 1.5 hrs; Port Townsend 2 2 hrs [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.59]
- Weyerhaeuser mill samples (avg / 95% CI): bleaching and dyeing 3900 / 1330; PT1 6060 / 5800; PT2 8920 / 17360; T277 12540 / 7690 [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.60]
- Inland mill samples (avg / 95% CI): bleaching and dyeing 1720 / 100; PT1 1940 / 3830; PT2 5100 / 21000; T277 21930 / 128330. Units are not labelled on the slide; the SPAN columns are garbled in the extract [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.60]
- Note: methods give very different absolute counts on the same samples, so compare only within one method.

## Micro stickies methods
- Research tests: deposition on polyethylene bottle or film, solvent extraction (requires screening), Pulmac macro and micro method (US Patent 7674355) [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.63]
- Patent method: screen and weigh macrostickies on the Pulmac; retain the material that passed; agglomerate the microstickies; screen the agglomerates, remove and weigh them [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.67]
