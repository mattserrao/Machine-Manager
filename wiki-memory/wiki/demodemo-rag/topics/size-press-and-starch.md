- Sizing improves water resistance and surface properties; conventional size press comes in vertical, inclined, horizontal configurations [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.28]
- Conventional press limits at speed: pond turbulence and uneven pickup; gate roll press uses an offset pond and metering nip; metering (film transfer) press uses short-dwell applicators [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.29, p.30]
- Setback: hot starch pastes thicken on cooling; waxy starch (100% amylopectin) avoids setback but has lower sizing efficiency; modification or enzyme conversion gives thin-boiling starch [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.1]
- Traditional size press: low-viscosity starch about 4-10% solids, target pickup 70-110 lb per ton; calender stack pickup about 10 lb per ton [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.1]
- Batch cooking 88-93 C held 20-30 min; keep below boiling to prevent foaming; premodified starch normally used [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.1]
- Continuous eductor (jet) cooker heats by high-velocity steam; pressurized jet cooker can thermally convert unmodified starch [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.1]
- Calender sizing via a water box with reinforced rubber lip; more material goes on at the top of the stack; after-calender drying needed [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.1]
- Tub sizing (shallow bath, light nip, hot-air impingement drying) impregnates the sheet to improve ply bond, burst, stiffness, tensile; usually off-machine [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.2]
