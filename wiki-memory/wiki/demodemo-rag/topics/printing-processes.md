## What papermakers need to know
- Runnability = ability to run through the press without breaks; key properties strength, uniformity, freedom from defects [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.6]
- Printability = how well the paper reproduces the image: brightness, opacity, colour, gloss, smoothness, porosity, sizing [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.6]
- Direct/contact methods still make over 90% of commercial printing [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.6]

## Processes
- Letterpress (relief): dominant until the early 1960s; ink contains 8 to 12% carbon black in oil; drawbacks are squeeze-out, embossing, clogging of shallow relief [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.6] [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.7]
- Over 90% of newspapers are now printed by lithography and flexography; gravure is used mainly for high-quality magazines, supplements and inserts [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.6]
- Offset lithography: oil and water do not mix; image transfers plate to rubber blanket to paper, which suits rough paper; papers must be lint-free with good surface strength; fibres on the blanket force press shutdowns for cleaning [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.7]
- Gravure: etched copper cells, doctor blade, high plate cost and long make-ready so only high-volume work; chrome-plated cylinders run millions of impressions; needs very smooth paper (electrostatic assist can help rougher paper) [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.8]
- Flexography: relief process, fluid water-based inks, polymer plates, light pressure, anilox roll; quality between letterpress and offset, lower rub-off, less show-through, better deinking compatibility [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.8]
- Other: di-litho (1970s newspaper conversion), screen printing, driography (1970, waterless litho, withdrawn for toning problems) [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.8]

## Colour and press operation
- Colour halftones are screened through blue, green and red filters; press prints yellow, magenta, cyan plus usually black, each at a different screen angle [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.9]
- Modern four-colour offset uses stacked towers with blanket-to-blanket couples; short distance between couples reduces registration errors [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.9]
- High-speed presses handle tension control, flying paster reel splicing, register control, ink drying; longer runs need higher web tension [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.9]
- Press web breaks usually come from sheet defects combined with low paper strength or high web tension; the defect is often visible at the tear as a sliver or shive [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.10]
