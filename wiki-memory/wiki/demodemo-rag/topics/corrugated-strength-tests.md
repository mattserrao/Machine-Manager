- "Compressive strength is the single most important requirement for corrugated boxes, related to the edgewise compressive strength (ECT)"; ECT depends largely on compressive properties of liners and fluting [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.5]
- Tests listed: Concora medium test (CMT), ring crush test (RCT), STFI (short span compression), edgewise compression test (ECT) [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.5]
- Ring crush: traditional test of linerboard and corrugating medium; measures compression resistance, related to the eventual compression strength of combined board; high performance linerboard reaches a minimum ring crush at lower basis weights [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.7]
- Concora crush: three tests (flat crush of lab-fluted material, edgewise strength of a short column of board, edgewise strength of a lab-fluted strip parallel to flute tips) [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.7]
- Bursting strength: combined tensile and stretch resisting rupture under pressure; "its value in determining the potential permanence or durability of paper is suspect" [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.7]
