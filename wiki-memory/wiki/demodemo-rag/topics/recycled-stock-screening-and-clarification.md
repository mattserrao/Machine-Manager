## Screening
- Principles: "Do not mix a clean stream with a dirty stream." and "Avoid recirculation of contaminants." [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.26]
- Conventional cascade: each stage's rejects feed the next, with returns looping back to the previous stage's inlet [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.27]
- Forward flow (two stage): first box 0.008" slots, its rejects go to a second box with 0.006" slots, second box accepts rejoin the accept line; final rejects "Or higher reject ratio" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.28]
- Forward flow with three or four boxes: each stage's accepts join the accept line via valve junction; last rejects exit [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.29]
- Pressure screens break and deform adhesives, lowering screening efficiency; shredding makes particles more one-dimensional [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.24]
- Through-flow cleaner removes low density contaminants; a reverse cleaner has a smaller top middle reject port and a wider bottom cone tip for accepts [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.31]

- Smook: screen slots went from 0.008" (early 1990s) to 0.006" to 0.004"; micro-stickies agglomerate later, so some plants moved final fine screens to the end or added a set ahead of the dryer. See [[secondary-fiber-supply-and-recycling-effects]]. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.13] [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.15]

## Clarification
- Clarifier takes filtrate and makes sludge and clarified filtrate: feed 2000 ppm suspended solids, clarified water 100 ppm, sludge 3-7% solids, no change in colloidal or dissolved species [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.33]
- DAF (dissolved air flotation) clarifier: raw water and chemicals in, floated sludge scooped off the top, settled sludge from the bottom, air and recycled water through a pressurization vessel [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.34]
- Tissue/recycled stock prep layouts: single-layer (Fig 10.2) and twin-layer (Fig 10.3) options, with WW1/WW2 white water loops [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.48]
- Rejects and sludges by grade (% dry weight), e.g. hygienic paper white water clarification sludge 15-25, flotation deinking sludge 8-13; liner/fluting total 4-9 [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.44]
