## Table (one row per symptom)
- Uneven wear, frequent blade change: causes poor roll surface, damaged or dirty blade, deposits, poor lubrication shower on wire or press parts; remedies use a more flexible blade or holder, clean/repair/replace holder, use abrasive blade on cast iron surface, ensure lubrication shower nozzles give correct fan coverage [DOC 5d797427-af61-43ca-9fb8-4f8bcd8261ee p.1]
- Excessive wear in center of blade or at ends: causes holder not profiled correctly, old doctor sagging into or away from roll; remedies refit and shim holder to roll profile, install new doctor back [DOC 5d797427-af61-43ca-9fb8-4f8bcd8261ee p.1]
- Excessive wear one end: cause doctor misaligned, not parallel to roll axis; remedy shim or move bearings and brackets to align parallel to axis [DOC 5d797427-af61-43ca-9fb8-4f8bcd8261ee p.1]
- Even wear but rough edge: causes deposit on roll, excessive pressure on blade, blade material too hard; remedies use abrasive blade as required, reduce pressure, use softer blade [DOC 5d797427-af61-43ca-9fb8-4f8bcd8261ee p.1]
- Excessive wear in sheet run: causes deposits left on roll by sheet, insufficient lubrication on a press application; remedies use abrasive blade as required, trim blade edges frequently, "Use end slotted blades to" (text cut off in the source) [DOC 5d797427-af61-43ca-9fb8-4f8bcd8261ee p.1]
