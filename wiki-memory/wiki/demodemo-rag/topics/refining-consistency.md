## Recommended range of refining consistency (J&L Fiber Services)
- Unbleached softwood kraft 3.5 - 4.5%. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.27]
- Bleached softwood kraft 3.5 - 5%. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.27]
- Bleached hardwood/eucalyptus kraft 4 - 6%. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.27]
- OCC 3.5 - 5%. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.27]
- Mechanical pulps 4.5 - 6%; unbleached semi-chem hardwood 4.5 - 6%; mixed waste 4 - 6%. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.27]

## Smook (other source)
- Higher consistency gives more fiber-to-fiber contact and less fiber-to-metal cutting, improving beating response. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.30]
- Hollander beater runs 4-8%; in-line dispersers up to 4.5%; see [[refining-mechanisms-and-variables]], [[pulpers-and-repulping]]. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.26]

## Why it matters
- Proper consistency raises probability of fiber mat formation, maximises fiber strength potential and plate life, minimises variation. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.26]
- High consistency: plate plugging, poor fiber development. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.28]
- Low consistency: little to no fiber mat, inefficient refining, poor fiber development, fiber cutting, plate clashing, short plate life. [DOC 856ff3f6-9be4-42e7-b4dc-fee63dfd3cfd p.28]
