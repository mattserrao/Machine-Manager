Source: [[doc-smook-fundamentals-part-07]] pp.10-11. Related: [[chlorine-dioxide-bleaching]], [[mechanical-pulp-brightening]].

- Peroxide temperature roles: up to 60 C mechanical pulp brightening; 70-80 C chemical pulp full-bleach sequences; 90-100 C primary delignification with poor selectivity [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.10]
- Peroxide target pH about 10.5; above that side reactions reduce efficiency; silicate (often with MgSO4) stabilizes and buffers; EDTA or DTPA chelants hold back Mn, Cu, Fe [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.10]
- Peroxide reinforcement in the second extraction stage saves a larger equivalent of ClO2 downstream [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.10]
- PO stage (peroxide plus pressurized oxygen) is an efficient TCF brightening method [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.10]
- Ozone: acidic, pH 2-4, charge 0.5-1.0% on pulp; most feasible after an oxygen stage; MC ozonation simpler than HC [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.11]
- DZ stage (ozone added to D0) improves economy and lowers AOX; attractive when ClO2 generating capacity is limited [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.11]
- Xylanase on kraft brownstock can cut ClO2 need by 15-20%; typically two hours in high-density storage [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.11]
