## Costs and profit
- Variable (direct) costs: wood chips, chemicals, steam. Fixed (period) costs: operating labor, maintenance, salaries, taxes, insurance, depreciation. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.21]
- Incremental tonnage above baseline is mainly driven by direct costs, so it is very profitable and gives a strong incentive to raise productivity. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.21]
- Fig 12-6: fixed costs about $30,000+/day flat; breakeven at 275 tpd (axis 0-600 tpd); below it a loss, above it profit. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.21]
- Fig 12-7: baseline bar includes fixed costs and depreciation; the incremental bar has only fiber, chemicals and energy, so the profit slice is much larger. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.21]
- Incremental investment: true costs are often underestimated; removing a washing bottleneck raises evaporation load on the pulp dryer and affects the recovery furnace. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.23]

## Inventory
- New mills may stock 3-4 times normal pulpwood/chip inventory before startup. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.21]
- Inventory control matters most when demand is soft; when demand is high the mill produces to fill orders. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.21]

## Cost-effectiveness, integration, cost control
- Obsolescence depends on profitability, not only age; management criteria: energy efficiency, raw material cost, manhours per unit, overhead, capital cost. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.22]
- Integration lowers unit cost by sharing power stations, wood handling, water and effluent treatment; integrating pulp with papermaking eliminates pulp transport. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.22]
- Budgets set standards; variance analysis should focus on why costs changed, not what changed; a fall in unit cost can hide higher usage, so track unit cost and usage. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.22]
- A 10% cut in steam cost has the same effect on pulp cost as a 10% cut in steam usage. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.23]
- Capital request answers four questions: current situation and deficiency; alternatives; pros and cons; recommendation and why. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.23]
- Energy balance: compare inputs and outputs per mill section to find wasted energy; check waste-heat recycle and boiler thermal efficiency. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.23]
