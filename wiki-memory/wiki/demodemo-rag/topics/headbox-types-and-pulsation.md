## Function
- Headbox converts fan pump pipeline flow into an even rectangular discharge, machine width, uniform velocity in MD. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.4]
- Five objectives: spread stock evenly; reduce cross-currents and consistency variation; reduce MD velocity gradients; controlled turbulence against flocculation; discharge evenly and hit the fabric at the right point and angle. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.4]
- Jet speed: "v = √(2gh)" (v m/s, h head m, g 9.81 m/s2). The jet contracts and bends downward after the slice, so slice opening alone does not give jet thickness or flow. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.4]

## Types
- Open (gravity head): early machines, now mostly slow pulp machines; Voith open box for pulp and board machines up to 200 m/min (Fig 16-8). [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.5]
- Pressurized: hydraulic (jet speed set by feed pump pressure) or air-cushioned (pond under air pressure damps fluctuations). [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.4]
- Air-cushioned: rotating perforated rectifier rolls create turbulence and flatten velocity gradients; shower sprays on pond and sides; throat gap too large keeps inlet velocity streams, too small creates eddies. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.5]
- Hydraulic: perforated rolls used sparingly; shear forces and small tubes and channels break up fiber networks. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.5]

## Pulsation
- Some pressure variation can remain after fan pump and screens and cause MD basis weight variation. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.5]
- Hydraulic boxes lack an air cushion, so a surge tank (Fig 16-14) is added; pulsation energy becomes compression work at the air-suspension interface. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.5]
## Flowspreader, slice, bleeds, CD control (doc pages 6-11)
- Flowspreader: multitube tapered inlet manifold with recirculation (J. Mardon, mid-1950s); recirculation set so pressures at A and B match, no flow in the sight glass. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.6]
- Too little recirculation: header pressure rises along the header; too much: it falls (Fig 16-16). [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.8]
- Perforated rolls: hole 2-4 cm, open area 35-50%, 6-15 rpm. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.6]
- Slice: top lip moves as a whole, with local micro-adjusters; jet angle depends on L/b, near zero above L/b of about 2. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.7] [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.10]
- Jet-to-wire ratio kept close to 1; jet speed inferred from headbox pressure. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.10]
- Dry line and edge bleeds: properly set main and secondary bleeds give a nearly straight dry line; no bleed or wrong main bleed gives voids and distortion. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.8] [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.11]
- CD basis weight: slice screws distort lip opening and fibre orientation; dilution (white water at many points, Voith mixing modules) now standard on new headboxes. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.11]
- Consistency adjusted by raising or lowering the slice; stock addition only by the basis weight valve. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.9]
