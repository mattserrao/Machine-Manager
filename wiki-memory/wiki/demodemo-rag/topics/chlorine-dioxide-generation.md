Source: [[doc-smook-fundamentals-part-07]] pp.2-3. Use of ClO2 in bleaching: [[chlorine-dioxide-bleaching]]; chemical supply overview: [[bleaching-chemical-preparation]].

- ClO2 liquifies at 11 C, is unstable and explosive in pure form; mixtures below 20% give only mild "puffs" [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.2]
- Always made at the mill: generated from sodium chlorate in highly acidic solution, absorbed in cold water to "about 7 g/liter" [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.2]
- Table 11-2 (reducing agent / diluent): Solvay methanol / air; Mathieson SO2 / air; R-2 NaCl / air; R-3/SVP NaCl / water vapor [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.2]
- Solvay and Mathieson (1950s) are hard to control with poor conversion; R3/SVP is the newer, widely used process and produces only sodium sulfate crystals as by-product [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.2]
- Processes using NaCl as reducing agent (R2, R3/SVP) co-produce chlorine gas [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.2]
- R7 reacts by-product chlorine with SO2 and water to make HCl/H2SO4 feed; R8 uses methanol and crystallizes sodium sesquisulfate; R10 sends neutral saltcake to the recovery cycle [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.2]
- Mathieson: acid normality 9-10, chlorate 20-30 g/L, 35-40 C; "2NaClO3 + SO2 -> 2ClO2 + Na2SO4" [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.2]
- R3: vacuum generator with heat exchanger circulation, "2NaClO3 + 2NaCl + 2H2SO4 -> 2ClO2 + Cl2 + 2Na2SO4 + H2O"; saltcake crystallizes and is filtered [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.2]
- Saltcake and waste acid link to [[recovery-boiler]] makeup and [[kraft-byproducts-turpentine-tall-oil]] acidulation
