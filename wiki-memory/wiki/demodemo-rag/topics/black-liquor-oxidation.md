- Recovery steps: strong liquor by multiple-effect evaporators, oxidation if required, heavy liquor, recovery furnace, green liquor, causticizing, lime burning [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.28]
- Reactions: "2Na2S + 2O2 + H2O -> Na2S2O3 + 2NaOH" and "2NaHS + 2O2 -> Na2S2O3 + H2O" [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.28]
- Started in the 1950s as odor reduction; otherwise H2S is stripped by hot gases in the direct-contact evaporator [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.28]
- Oxidation is costly and robs fuel value; most furnaces since 1975 have no direct-contact evaporator so need none [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.28]
- Strong-liquor oxidation preferred later: smaller volume, negligible sulfide reformation (weak liquor oxidation reforms some via elemental sulfur) [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.28]
- Weak black liquor leaves washers at 13-17% solids; heavy liquor burned at 60-80%; 5-7 lb water per lb dry solids to evaporate [DOC 5964733d-306e-4207-a72d-c3ebb900a35e p.28]
