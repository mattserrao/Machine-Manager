## Selection and gas-phase control
- Only particulates and gases/vapours need real consideration; Table 27-5 lists equipment; Tables 27-6 and 27-7 list data needed before choosing [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.29]
- Thermal incineration about 650-800 C; catalytic combustion about 300-500 C (higher capital, lower operating cost, little used in pulp and paper) [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.29]
- Adsorption (activated carbon, silica gel) is rarely used in pulp and paper; suits very dilute or valuable gases [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.30]

## Wet scrubbers
- Commercial pressure drops range from 3 to over 100 inches water gauge; small particles and low-solubility gases need high pressure drop [DOC e24788a9-3652-41e0-a461-6f09e5cc575c p.30]
- Table 27-8 lists 11 categories (plate column, stationary packing, moving bed, fibrous packing, preformed spray, gas-atomized spray/Venturi, centrifugal, baffle, impingement-entrainment, mechanically aided, combination) [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.1]
- Venturi: smaller throat gives higher pressure drop and better sub-micron removal; followed by cyclonic separator and optionally a mesh mist eliminator [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.1]

## Mechanical collectors
- Settling chamber separates only particles above about 50 microns [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.1]
- Multi-tube cyclones remove particles as small as 5 microns at efficiencies approaching 95% [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.1]
- Cyclone efficiency can be improved by sending about 10% of gas flow as a secondary flow to a small scrubber (shave-off, Fig. 27-23) [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.2]

## Fabric, gravel bed, ESP
- Fabric filter: removes particles down to 0.1 micron; dry nonagglomerating dust to about 300 C; baghouse compartments cleaned in rotation by shaker, reverse air or pulse jet [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.3]
- Gravel bed filter: moving pea-size gravel bed, needs higher pressure drop than fabric filters [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.3]
- ESP: removal exceeds 99.9% down to about 0.1 micron; about 100 kV; about 50 mA per 1000 ft2 of collecting surface; gradient about 4 or 5 kV/cm [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.4]
- ESP is the long-standing standard for recovery boiler soda fume; also used on lime kiln and bark boiler exhaust [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.4]
- 1985 lime kiln evaluation (Fig. 27-26, Pollock and Walker): ESP needs large gas flows to justify its capital cost; at higher flows its lower operating cost offsets it [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.4] [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.6]
- ESP efficiency depends on exposure time and dust resistivity; moderate resistivity dust is easiest to collect [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.6]
- Hybrids: electrified filter bed, wet electrostatic precipitator, wet ESP plus packed-bed scrubber (Figs. 27-29 to 27-31) [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.6] [DOC 3f3ab0e9-c679-43a4-b486-19219a1e5c28 p.7]
