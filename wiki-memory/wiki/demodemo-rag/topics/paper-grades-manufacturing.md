## Grade overview
- Newsprint is almost one-third of paper production; linerboard plus corrugating medium is over half of paperboard; Lockwood-Post's lists over 200 North American products [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.24]
- Table 20-1 defines printing grades (newsprint, catalog, rotogravure, publication, banknote, bible, bond/ledger, stationery), industrial grades (bag, linerboard, corrugating medium, construction, greaseproof, glassine) and tissues [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.24]
- Corrugating medium: fluted inner plies, high-yield semichemical hardwood at about nine points [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.24]
- Condenser tissue about 5 g/m2; wrapping tissue 16-28 g/m2 [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.25]

## Publication grades
- SC-A: brightness 68-70, filler up to 35%, chemical pulp up to 30%, intensive supercalendering; SC-B up to 25% recycled fibre, soft-nip finish; SC-C filler 8-15% [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.28]
- MFP: light pigment coat applied at the size press, fills the gap between SC and LWC [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.28]
- Coated publication grades numbered 1 (best) to 5; basis weight 47-103 g/m2; brightness: No.1 82-88, No.2 78-82, No.3 76-82, No.4 72-78, No.5 68-72 (Table 20-5) [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.28]
- Magazines take about 40% of coated output; grades 4 and 5 are about 94% of coated magazine paper; below about 58 g/m2 both base sheet and coating must be reduced [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.28]

## Sack grades
- Tensile energy absorption (TEA) is the key property in a butt drop; TEA is the area under the stress-strain curve, roughly tensile strength x stretch at rupture [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.29]
- Headbox consistency typically 0.2-0.3%; use suction pickup and delay first open draw until after the second or third press to keep stretch [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.29]
- Air impingement drying (Flakt) applied at 50-85% dryness, where most free shrinkage occurs [DOC 8b9baf40-fc3e-4d72-974a-2d9e679268cc p.29]

## Linerboard
- Bottom liner from high-yield kraft, top liner from lower-yield well-refined unbleached kraft; top liner lightly sized at the calender stack [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.1]
- Dual-line pulping: base stock cooked to 55-60% yield (hot-stock refined plus de-shive refining), top liner cooked to about 48-50% yield; screen rejects go to the base line [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.1]
- Alternative: single line with fractionating screen to extract a clean short-fibre top liner stock; saves capital but slight yield loss [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.1]

## Corrugating medium
- Standard 9-point medium (26 lb/1000 ft2) is over 80% of production; weight range 18-36 lb/1000 ft2; typical 65% mixed-hardwood semichemical and 35% OCC; 100% OCC is called "bogus medium" [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.1]
- Needs stiffness and crush resistance; tensile, burst, tear mostly unimportant [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.2]
- Corrugating runability means surviving corrugating without flute fracture: high stretch and no large shives [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.2]

## Fine paper
- Fine paper is uncoated printing and writing with no more than 25% mechanical pulp; speeds may vary from 300 to 1000 m/min with frequent grade changes [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.2]
- Scheduling: same-furnish grades in sequence, cleanest brightest grades after a wash-up, lower-quality grades just before a shutdown [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.2]
- Typical furnish: light to moderate refining, 10-15% filler, alkaline wet end; dandy roll for formation, patterns or watermarks [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.3]
