## Source and limits
- Paper Machine Clothing 2nd ed. section 8.1; lists are guidance, no corrective actions or numeric values [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.1]

## Symptom lists (areas to consider, abbreviated)
- Barring: roll condition (vibration), couch roll seals, vibration on all elements, loose bearing housings, compare sheet mark to fabric pattern, pressure pulses from headbox, pressure screens, stock delivery [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.1]
- Cockling: poor formation, water/stock jumps, suction couch water circulation, reduce refining, stock pulsations, rewetting from showers, dandy or couch press water spots, dryer felt cleanliness [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.1]
- Couch shadow marking: drainage (may be flooding couch), vacuums, rush/drag or jet/wire ratio, low fabric tension, fabric wear, fabric drainage capacity, stock freeness too low [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.1]
- Cracks: machine deckles, trim, edge rewetting, stock slinging at roll edges, deckle wave, edge weight profile [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.1]
- Crushing: too wet at top wire unit, combining roll or into press; hydraulic pressure build-up on twin wire former; fabric cleanliness and tension; formation; draws; couch press; dandy roll balance/too hard; stock consistency too high; plugged fabric; suction couch shower setting [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.2]
- Curl: fibre alignment, two-sidedness, refining, draw, edge dewatering, long fibre proportion, freeness, retention, slice opening, uneven drying or coating pick-up [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.2]
- Dandy roll marks: plugged dandy shower, damaged/unlevel/unaligned dandy, sheet too wet or too dry, suction box vacuum [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.2]
- Edge breaks: "Trim squirt plugged - not cutting clean", recouching of sheet and edge trim, trim too small, dirt build-up on bottom lip or fabric edge, stock build-up on deckles, poor edge dewatering, rewetting at couch, damaged fabric, deckle board set-up, stock build-up on return rolls [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.2]
- Guiding: high suction box vacuum, guide palm sensitivity, uneven front-to-back tension, guide roll wrap/lead-in/lead-out, drive synchronization, slippage, doctor blades, bearings, unbalanced rolls [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.3]
- Moisture profile uneven: headbox slice, jet speed differences, rectifier roll, breast roll deflection, foils, fabric tension, shower water supply, plugged suction rolls, gap former jet delivery, top wire blade wear, high pressure showers plugged or not oscillating, breast roll shower temperature differential, headbox temperature compensator, profiler-to-scanner mapping [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.3]
- Pinholes: air in system, excessive drainage (stapling), slow table drainage, fabric open area, suction box vacuum too high, thin spots, sheet release [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.3]
- Ply bonding/delamination: increase refining, improve formation, decrease internal sizing, increase long fiber content [DOC 004f7fc0-956b-4eb3-aa93-df1adf3f0adf p.3]
