- Four directions MD, CD, residual R, ZD exist simultaneously; always state the wavelengths included. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.3-4]
- MD bands: long >200 s, medium 200-1 s, short 1 s-100 mm, formation <100 mm; 40% of variance sat in 2.5-25 Hz in the example. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.3-4]
- A beta gauge (about 1 s response) at 700 m/min misses wavelengths under 11.7 m. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.3]
- True CD profile needs reel samples; 10 plies standard; CD changes reel to reel. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.4, p.6]
- COV falls as data boxes get larger (2.15% at 13 mm to 1.77% at 150 mm); COV below 0.1% did not guarantee quality, a streak index was needed. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.7]
- Break model: breaks occur where stress-carrying ability and imposed stress distributions overlap; lower variation allows a lower strength target. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.1-2]
- ZD: fines and filler move with drainage water; twin-wire sheets fines-lean centre; excessive press load can case-harden a sheet. [DOC 6a85a636-1e70-46d5-af7e-3dc0beae290f p.8]
