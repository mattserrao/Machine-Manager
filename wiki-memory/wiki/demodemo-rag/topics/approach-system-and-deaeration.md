## Approach system (16.2)
- Definition: "the fan pump loop in which the papermaking furnish is metered, diluted, mixed with additives, screened, and cleaned before it reaches the forming fabric". Thick stock = machine chest stock; thin stock = diluted. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.2]
- Runs from machine chest to headbox slice. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.2]
- Fine papers and coated grades need nearly contaminant-free stock: fine slotted screens, better secondary and tertiary screen efficiency; screens tuned for high efficiency can plug, sometimes for days or weeks. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.2]
- Fan pump is the largest pump in the system; flow and pressure must be pulsation-free yet adjustable; two pumps in series when air is removed under vacuum. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.2]
- Traditional feed: constant-head stuff box with a basis weight valve on a straight pipe; newer machines drop the stuff box; a speed-controlled pump at the machine chest is faster and more accurate than valve control. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.2]

## Air and deaeration
- Air in stock causes holes, thin spots, poor drainage and approach instability; main source is free-draining white water picking up air in the forming section. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.3]
- Long open-channel flow in the wire pit lets air escape; defoamers may help. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.3]
- Deaeration: stock (typically first-stage centricleaner accepts) sprayed into a vacuum tank so free and dissolved gases flash off (Valmet tank Fig 16-4; Deculator Fig 16-5). Stock and white water can share one vacuum compartment (Fig 16-6). [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.3]
- Pomp centrifugal pump removes almost all entrained air from wire-pit water via a vortex chamber but not dissolved air, which is harmless while dissolved. [DOC 66857f06-84b5-470f-ada6-025ffe226134 p.3]

## Effect of air on drainage (Hubbe 2020)
- 0.4-0.8% air by volume roughly doubles dewatering time; headbox air up to 4%: see [[entrained-air-and-drainage]] [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.50]
