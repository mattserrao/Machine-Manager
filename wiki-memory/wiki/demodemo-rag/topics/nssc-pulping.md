## Process
- Yield 65-85%; usually hardwood (birch) for high hemicellulose, "20-25% xylan"; 10-20% kraft softwood often mixed in for wet strength and runability [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.20]
- Chip impregnation in sodium sulfite and carbonate/bicarbonate 5-15 min at 100 C [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.20]
- Cook pH starts 7-10, drops to 5-6; charge "7-10% sodium sulfite and 2-4% sodium hydroxide, based on dry wood"; cooking time 5-60 min, 160-200 C [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.21]
- Defibration (primary refining) at high temperature and pressure; beating (secondary refining) at low/medium consistency [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.21]
- Kraft for comparison: yield about 50%, pH 14 falling to 10-12, 160-180 C, 1-2 h [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.21-22]

## Board use and formulas
- Fluting is NSSC (needs compression strength SCT/CCT and tensile stiffness); liner is often kraft softwood [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.23]
- Box compression strength by McKee: BCT = k1 * ECT^0.75 * (S_B,MD * S_B,CD)^0.25 * Z^0.5 (Eq.5; ECT edgewise compression kN/m, S_B bending stiffness Nm, Z box perimeter m) [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.23]
- Paper machine stock concentration normally about 0.6%; solids about 20% end of wire, 40-50% after presses [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.22]
- Higher pH of NSSC at Gruvon "gives superior paper properties" per earlier trials; thesis tests this: [[fiber-ph-swelling-and-strength]] [DOC 6e312421-09ec-48b8-97ec-a68bbff246e8 p.11]
