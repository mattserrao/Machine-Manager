## Standards and precision
- Standards bodies (Table 22-1): TAPPI, PAPTAC, SCAN, ASTM, PITA, APPITA [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.16]
- Test terms: sensitivity, readability, precision, accuracy; replicates specified per test in TAPPI, SCAN, APPITA (Table 22-7) [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.17] [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.18]
- Round-robin testing and inter-laboratory reference system for inter-mill comparison [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.19]

## Conditioning
- Standard North American conditions 23 C and 50% relative humidity; conditioned paper typically 7-9% moisture [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.24]
- Moisture depends on whether paper came from a drier or moister state (hysteresis, Figure 22-8) [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.24]
- Roll paper such as newsprint is often tested "as made" and kept sealed until testing [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.24]
- Paper is viscoelastic and hygroscopic, so results depend on loading rate and moisture history [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.24]

## Sides and direction
- Fourdrinier paper coarser/more open on the wire side; twin-wire paper usually more even; wire-mark penetration for 90-micron newsprint usually 10-30 microns [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.25]
- Brightness is measured on the top side and roughness on the wire side [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.25]
- Tensile and folding strength higher in MD, tear higher in CD; MD:CD ratio fourdrinier 1.5 to 2.0, cylinder machine up to 5.0 or more [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.25]
- Random-sheet value is the geometric mean: X(random) = sqrt(X_MD x X_CD) [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.26]
- A wetted sample curls with its curl axis in the MD [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.26]

## Table 22-11 (TAPPI numbers)
- Grammage T410, caliper T411, folding endurance (MIT) T423, tear T470 (mN m2/g), burst T403 (kPa m2/g), tensile breaking length T404, stiffness (Gurley) T451, stretch T457, TEA T494 [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.26]
- Roughness (Sheffield) T479, Dennison wax pick T459, sizing T466, water absorbency (Cobb) T441, grease resistance T454, air resistance T460, water vapour permeability T448 [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.26]
- Cobb sizing test: contact time usually 5 minutes, result in g/m2 [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.27]
- Elmendorf tear uses a falling pendulum; good fibre strength indicator but questioned as a predictor of pressroom runnability [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.27]
- Brightness is reflectance relative to magnesium oxide at 457 nm [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.27]

## Aging
- Accelerated aging in an oven at 105 C; brightness reversion recommended 4 hours (TAPPI SM-200) [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.27]
- Heat test TAPPI T-453: 72 hours at 105 C, folding endurance retention as heat-treated value divided by original [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.28]

## On-line testing
- Grammage, moisture and caliper routinely monitored on-line; sensors exist for brightness, colour, gloss, opacity, smoothness, ash, coat weight, formation; off-line testing still needed for investigations and validating sensors [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.28]
## AFT manual paper tests ([[doc-aft-intro-stock-prep-refining]])
- Nearly all paper properties are a function of density, largely set by refining; refining raises density, tensile, burst; lowers porosity and opacity; often lowers tear. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.17] [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.12]
- Ring crush strip 1/2 in x 6 in; STFI short span test; compressive strength rises nearly linearly with density; Scott Bond for internal bond. [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.18]
- Ash test at 525 C (or 900 C depending on fillers). [DOC 573e2f9c-afca-42a5-b4a6-10980694ab2e p.19]
- Compressive strength vs density: see [[compressive-strength-density-and-bonding]].
