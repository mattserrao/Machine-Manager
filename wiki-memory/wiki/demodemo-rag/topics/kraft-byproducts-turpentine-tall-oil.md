- Turpentine is recovered from digester relief vapors: cyclone, condenser, decanter, storage [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.22]
- List prices ranged $0.60-2.00 per gallon turpentine and $90-270 per ton crude tall oil; mills may burn them as fuel when prices are low [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.21]
- Tall oil soap solubility is minimum at 25-30% solids; liquor is concentrated through 2 or 3 effects then pumped to a skimming tank [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.22]
- 0.5-1.0% air by volume at the skimmer pump inlet can raise crude tall oil recovery by up to 10%; lignosulfonate additives claim up to 13% [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.22]
- Acidulation with waste sulfuric acid (often from the ClO2 plant) converts soap to raw tall oil; brine returns to the black liquor system [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.22]
- Table 10-5 yield lb/ton pulp: Southern States 90, Mid-Atlantic 80, Canada 40, S. Western 125, East of Cascades 50, West of Cascades 30 [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.23]
