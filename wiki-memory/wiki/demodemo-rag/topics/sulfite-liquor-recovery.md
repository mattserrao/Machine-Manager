Source: [[doc-smook-fundamentals-part-06]] (book pp.160-163). Kraft counterpart: [[recovery-boiler]].

- Calcium base liquors allow only heat recovery (CaSO4 forms); ammonium base allows heat and sulfur recovery but no base recovery [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.24]
- Magnesium base: no smelt; chemicals leave as MgO ash and SO2 gas; 80-90% of ash removed by cyclone, rest scrubbed with Mg(OH)2 solution [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.24]
- Rauma: green liquor carbonated, "Na2CO3 + Na2S + 3CO2 + 3H2O -> 4NaHCO3 + H2S" [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.24]
- Tampella: "Na2S + Na2CO3 + 2CO2 + 2H2O -> NaHS + 3NaHCO3"; H2S burned with makeup sulfur to SO2 [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.26]
- Gasification: entrained-bed about 950 C or higher (molten smelt); fluidized-bed about 700 C or lower (dry solids); no practical process fully demonstrated per the text [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.26]
- Kraft recovery drawbacks: high capital cost, large equipment, smelt-water explosion hazard [DOC 0f514475-3eac-427f-bd93-d1e763e9fe34 p.26]
