## The method list (as given on the slides)
- Use of environmentally benign (recyclable) adhesive; avoidance via recovered paper quality control; mechanical removal (screening, cleaning); water clarification; chemical control (polymers, surfactants); passivation with inorganic particles; enzyme hydrolysis; physical adsorption to fibers (retention); dispersion; protection (treat equipment to limit deposits); continuous cleaning of clothing; water dumping; mill shut down for clean-up [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.14]

## Details by method
- Recovered paper quality: attributes tracked are stickies, dirt, groundwood, strength, coated, plastic films, brightness, glass/metal, municipal trash, uniformity [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.19]
- What to measure in bales: visually inspect bale outside for stickers, inspect after opening, track supplier/location/grade with high stickies, reject with justification or discuss with supplier [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.20]
- Pulper performance: often cannot change parameters; favoured gentle pulping, drum pulping, pre soaking, short pulping time, low temperature, low pH [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.22]
- Screening efficiency improves with lower consistency, lower pressure drop, lower temperature, lower passing velocity, feed forward vs cascade; pressure screens can shred adhesives into thousands of small particles [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.25]
- Micro stickies removal efficiency improves with better clarification: maximum air addition and retention time, proper type/dosage/mixing of chemicals, routine suspended solids testing, additional clarifier capacity [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.35]
- Additives: talc; zirconium compounds; cationic fixatives, anionic, nonionic surfactants, starch; enzymes that hydrolyze ester groups [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.37]
- Fixation on fibers: "Cationic polymers with high charge density and low MW used to fix anionic stickies to anionic fibers"; needs good mixing in stock prep and "passivation of papermaking equipment" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.39]
- Dispersion: stock to 30%, steam to 185-245 C; needs excellent washing and clarification right after and a pacifying additive "Otherwise, the problem will worsen for papermachine"; "Not recommended, energy intensive and harm to the fibers" [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.42]
- Clean-up: "The amount of residual cleaner in the paper machine water loop should be minimized if not reduced to zero concentration before start up"; cleaners are dispersants so residue can increase deposits; follow the cleaner manufacturer's instructions [DOC 66b13d8e-4a11-494d-9513-781badb05dab p.45]
- Recycled-grade tools to cut stickies: use talc or other inorganics, blend with virgin, improved processing [DOC c93229dc-2711-42b6-9211-821d0e0902e1 p.66]

## Smook handbook view
- Glues, hot melts and latexes are lumped as "stickies"; Table 14-3 lists effects (pressure sensitives stick to fabrics and felts and cause web breaks; hot melts foul equipment); dispersion systems nullify stickies by spreading them over fibers.
