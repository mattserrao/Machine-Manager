Source: [[doc-hubbe-rate-limiting-water-removal]] (p.58-61). Retention basics: [[wet-end-retention-and-zeta-potential]]. Related: [[dewatering-enhancement-strategies]], [[wet-end-additives-sizing-fillers-dyes]].

- Chemicals help when they give a bulky wet web with open channels; bulkiness correlates with dewatering rate (Gruber 1997; Kugge 2005; Hakovirta 2014) [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.58]
- Lab test: sediment volume test; debonding agents raised both sediment volume and dewatering rate (Kugge 2005); mechanism is higher effective fiber-fiber friction coefficient [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.58] [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.59]
- Same-sign charge repulsion lets fibers slide into a dense mat [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.59]
- Fines retention helps drainage; neutralising charge (near-zero zeta potential) or adding salt is "usually not sufficient" under shear [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.59]
- Success usually comes from very-high-mass acrylamide copolymers (bridging flocculation), effective at low dose and moderately high shear [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.59]
- Charged-patch: a high-charge, moderate-mass, often branched cationic polymer adsorbs in patches; works mainly early in dewatering; reversible after high shear [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.59]
- Nanofibrillated or microfibrillated cellulose acts like harmful fines and lowers dewatering rate; retention aid reduces the penalty (Balea 2019); NFC pretreated with high cationic starch then colloidal silica gave strength with low density and avoided the dewatering loss [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.60]
- Over-flocculation from very-high-mass retention aids reduces strength and vacuum dewatering; fix by choosing the addition point: dosing "before the pressure screen" lets screen rotor shear break fiber-fiber bridges while fiber-fines links partly survive, still a strong net drainage gain [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.60]
- Micro/nanoparticle programs (cationic starch or cationic acrylamide copolymer plus colloidal silica or bentonite) gave "the most impressive increases in dewatering rates" of non-enzymatic chemistry; widely used on printing grades [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.60] [DOC 7d704e0a-0ca7-4284-8fb9-dc6721c03b8a p.61]
- No dosage numbers (kg/t) are given in these pages.
