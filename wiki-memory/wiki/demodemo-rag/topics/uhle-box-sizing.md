## Sizing rules
- Excess uhle box slot area is found on 50 percent of systems studied; effects: reduced dewatering rate, felt shedding and damage, limited press exit solids [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.4]
- "TAPPI TIP 0404-27 describes details critical to uhle box design and application." [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- "Slot area is based only on machine speed. For example, a total slot width of 1" is required for a machine speed of 2500 fpm." [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- Extra dwell time has no benefit; it only adds vacuum pump capacity and power [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- Vacuum airflow per box = slot area x vacuum factor; "A vacuum factor range is 15-20 cubic feet per minute, per square inch of slot area"; sizes the box, piping, separator and vacuum source [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- Worked example: 250" wide, 2500 fpm, 1" total slot width requires 4000-5000 cfm per uhle box [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- Best practices list: "Minimize uhle box slots to not exceed 2 milliseconds of dwell time per uhle box"; "Maintain vacuum factors of 15 - 20 cfm/in"; verify herringbone slot areas are not excessive [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.6]

## One or two boxes per felt
- No practical reason for different uhle box designs or slot layouts within one press unless only part was upgraded [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- Two per felt is usually for felt cleaning, not extra dewatering; an existing third-press felt with two boxes can often drop one [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- If felts are not filled at end of life, a single uhle box is adequate; use press fabric water permeability and moisture scans to judge cleaning [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]
- Herringbone or zig-zag covers are seldom needed with seamed felts; 4000+ fpm machines run six and seven weeks on seamed felts with slotted covers [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5]

## Flow measurement
- Measure and trend uhle box and press nip flows: easiest method is existing seal tanks with V-notch weirs fitted with level transmitters calibrated to flow; one weir per felt [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.5-6]
- Trending water removal by source (uhle box vs nip) informs felt design, roll cover, chemical cleaning and nip dewatering decisions [DOC 56ec08f5-d829-4786-b810-b1a7de9c7f74 p.6]
