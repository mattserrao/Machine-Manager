Sources: [[doc-steam-condensate-for-paper-machines]]; Smook equivalent in [[dryer-condensate-siphons-and-blow-through]]. Context [[dryer-section-drying]], [[syphon-differential]].

- Square steel bars (can be hollow), usually 20-25 pieces, held to the shell by baskets and clamping rings; dryer dia ca 1450-1800 mm. [DOC f2d6b892-8f3a-49f6-bfb1-8f6beada12e7 p.30]
- Condensate is a poor heat conductor and acts as an insulator; bars create turbulence and extra surface that improve heat transfer. [DOC f2d6b892-8f3a-49f6-bfb1-8f6beada12e7 p.31]
- Regimes: "condensate collects ... as pit in the dryer" up to 350 m/min; above 350 m/min carried by friction; above 500 m/min a closed condensate ring forms (p.31 diagram: 0-300, 300-450, >450 m/min). [DOC f2d6b892-8f3a-49f6-bfb1-8f6beada12e7 p.31]
- Ring thickness ca 6-8 mm argued as right, set by syphon-to-shell distance; the syphon does not suck, condensate flows by centrifugal force. [DOC f2d6b892-8f3a-49f6-bfb1-8f6beada12e7 p.31]
- Results: production increase "about 5% - 8%", more uniform heat transfer and better moisture profile, "3-4 hours for the installation in one dryer". [DOC f2d6b892-8f3a-49f6-bfb1-8f6beada12e7 p.33]
- Not sensible on dryers 1-5 where a heating curve below 100 C is wanted; steam cooling of first group suggested instead. [DOC f2d6b892-8f3a-49f6-bfb1-8f6beada12e7 p.33]
- Smook: Beloit installation with 25 bars; segments at CD positions correct moisture profile. [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.12]
- Lost turbulator-base screws damage syphon shoes more than normal wear. [DOC f2d6b892-8f3a-49f6-bfb1-8f6beada12e7 p.20]
