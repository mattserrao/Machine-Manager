## Definitions and sheeting
- Finishing: winding, slitting, cutting, sorting, counting, cartoning, palletizing, wrapping; converting remanufactures dimensions, shape, surface or properties (not printing) [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.29]
- Folio sheets 11 x 17 in to 38 x 50 in; senior is larger; principal rotary cut size about 8.5 x 11 or 14 in [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.29]
- Sheeter: unwind stands up to a dozen rolls, slitters, revolving knife against fixed bed knife, sheet length set by web speed, layboys stack on moving belts [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.29]
- Duplex sheeter splits the web to two knives with independent speeds so two sheet lengths can run at once [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.30]
- Guillotine trimming used when size or edge appearance is critical; modern trimmers auto-reset the back stop [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.29]
- Precision sheeting ships untrimmed sheets; very sophisticated equipment, usually justified only for high-tonnage operations [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.30]
- Decurling: paper wound near the core tends to curl; up to 2% waste from excessively curled sheets [DOC 30d870b7-8b32-475a-9f65-e8f3bd87bd7b p.30] [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.1]
- Simplest decurler is a flat breaker bar set at a sharp angle opposite the curl; round bar protects coated surfaces; small free breaker roll suits heavy or scratch-prone sheets [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.1]

## Inspection, packaging, conditioning
- Automatic on-machine control of grammage, moisture, caliper, colour and formation reduced the need for later inspection; some mills inspect only when reel samples are out of tolerance [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.1]
- Optical fault detectors (Figure 23-4) can reject sheets with defects automatically [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.1]
- Larger sheets packed in cartons of about 150 lb (68 kg); skids replacing cartons, some customers place skids at the press feed end [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.1]
- Conditioning adds moisture to the roll web before cutting for flatness and colour register; widely used in Europe, not adopted in North America [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.1]
- Humidified air impinges on the web over drums; driest areas take most moisture, evening moisture and nearly eliminating static (Figure 23-6) [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.1]
- Embossing: engraved steel roll against a soft or compressible roll; a non-water alternative to watermarking [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.2]

## Finishing options
- Customer pressure for short turnarounds and small orders pushes mills toward off-site (mill-owned satellite) or outsourced finishing; outsourcing means no capital investment and fixed finishing costs [DOC 7aa5600d-4fda-4f9d-bb53-784faf0a0e24 p.2]
