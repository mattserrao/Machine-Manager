Source: [[doc-voith-press-felt-start-up]] (DOC 09275f87). Related: [[press-felt-construction-and-conditioning]] (Albany/Smook conditioning, HP shower 1.0-1.5 gpm at 1000 psig for traversing showers), [[press-section-design]], [[fabric-tension-measurement]].

- Sequence: square and tradeline parallel to CD; stretch roll to snug; jog with nip open (or at dead weight); stop and raise to normal tension; lube shower on and crawl at dead weight until uniformly wet; then normal load and Uhle box vacuum pump on [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.2-3]
- Run 15-20 minutes at speed before tailing [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.3]
- Start-up HP shower not above 200 psi (150 ideal) [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.3]
- Lube shower 15-25 psi, .04-.08 gpm/in.; HP shower 175-250 psi, .05-.10 gpm/in., oscillating [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.4]
- CONFLICT note on pressure basis: Smook/Lodding HP traversing shower figure is 1000 psig [DOC 212d6dd4-0711-4fdc-8e33-735d3a13d5de p.4]; Voith continuous HP shower is 175-250 psi [DOC 09275f87-d36f-41a5-af70-b22e0d0c57c7 p.4]. Different shower types/applications, not reconciled in sources.
