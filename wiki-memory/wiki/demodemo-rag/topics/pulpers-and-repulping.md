## Virgin pulp and broke (Ch.13)
- Repulping: mechanical action breaking dry pulp into slush; batch systems defiber fully in one vessel, continuous systems add in-line deflakers/dispergers. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.24]
- Unbleached kraft is hard to reslush once dried and stored; broke is easy unless it has wet-strength additives; waste papers cause the greatest problems. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.24]
- "Energy use is reduced by operating at the highest practical consistency (up to 18%) and at temperatures above 50°C." [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.24]
- High-consistency pulping 15-18%: more fiber-to-fiber friction, much lower energy; conical rotors fit the vat, guide vanes control fold-over. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.25]
- Hydrapulper (Black Clawson) extracts through a perforated plate, holes as small as 1/8 inch. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.25]
- In-line dispersion: stock up to 4.5% passes slits between rotor and stator rings; hydrodynamic shear loosens bonds and breaks flakes. [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.25]
- Bale handling: two-storey destacking and dewiring line (Fig 13-3); Escher Wyss deflaker (Fig 13-4/13-5). [DOC d9815fa5-40a2-474b-9932-eaf665991fc0 p.26]

## Wastepaper pulpers (Ch.14.3)
- Low-consistency pulper with ragger and junker runs at 5-8% consistency, continuous or batch; strings and wires leave as a "debris rope", heavy objects drop to the junking tower. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.11]
- High-consistency pulper keeps plastics and wet-strength flakes large for later removal, better ink removal, gentle on fibers; batch only, shorter batch times. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.11]
- Drum pulper (late 1980s): pulp at 15-20% consistency moves from pulping zone to screening zone with showers; high capital cost, less energy, gentle; installed in most new deinking plants. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.12]
- Betonniere: operates at 35-45% consistency, for the most difficult furnishes including wet-strength papers. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.12]
- In-line secondary pulpers (dispersers, fiberizers, deflakers) defiber further and separate heavy and light trash. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.13]
- "higher temperature will facilitate defibering ... Consistency should be maintained at the highest level that still gives good circulation." [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.13]
- Clean wastepaper system (Fig 14-7): pulper extraction plate, liquid cyclone, fine screen, rejects through deflaker back via secondary screening. Three-stage Voith-Morden system for dirty mixed waste (Fig 14-8). [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.13]
