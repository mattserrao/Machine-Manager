## Refining control
- Two goals: optimum strength, and control of drainability and formation; easiest and lowest energy in a two-stage system. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.6]
- Primary stage: low-intensity treatment of virgin fiber for strength, usually constant specific energy for the product, using consistency and flow transmitters plus a kW meter. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.6]
- Size refiners no larger or faster than expected throughput requires, to save energy. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.6]
- Secondary stage: whole furnish (virgin, pre-refined, secondary, broke) refined just before the machine; "High-intensity refining at relatively low specific energy gives the best results here." [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.6]
- Freeness held constant by feedback from a drainage-time or wetness-permeability sensor installed as close to the refiner as possible (Fig 13-26); error signal adjusts the refiner motor load set point. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.6]
- Net energy and intensity definitions: [[specific-refining-energy]], [[specific-edge-load]].

## Metering and blending (13.3)
- Described as "the most under-rated function in stock preparation"; furnish variation shows up as erratic stock behavior on the wire, fluctuating wet web response and variable quality. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.6]
- Components usually metered into the machine chest at 2.8 to 3.2% consistency; deflaking and refining at intermediate consistency. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.7]
- Modern mills: continuous, automated, consistency loops and magnetic flow meters driving control valves; each component stream ratioed to total flow demand. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.7]
- Fig 13-27: broke, chemical and mechanical pulp percentages as targets; additives (dye, starch) dosed in kg/t; consistency fine-tuned on the combined flow leaving the blend chest. Newsprint often omits the blend chest. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.7]

## Machine chest to headbox
- Machine chest holds the final furnish at 2.6 to 2.8% consistency, circulated to a constant-head stuff box. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.8]
- Stock passes the basis weight valve into the approach system, mixes with wire pit white water; consistency drops to about 1% or less at the headbox. [DOC 01a335fe-bfba-4f11-b32c-df2de03d8521 p.8]
