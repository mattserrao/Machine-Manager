How answers are scored
  • Correct (35%): any wrong value, setting, step, date or id scores 1 at most; invented facts score 0.
  • Cited (35%): every specific claim needs an inline citation to something a tool returned in that run. Each citation is checked by code against the full tool output; one that isn't found counts as fabricated, sets Cited to 0 and caps the answer at 50.
  • Complete (15%): share of the answer key's must-have points covered.
  • Concise (15%): leads with the answer, about 150 words for knowledge and calculation questions, 220 for data questions and briefs.
  • Questions: 54 in three sections (papermaking, industrial / chemical engineering, Sim Mill data), 12 train and 6 held-out each.
